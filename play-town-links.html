Maps Within Maps

A new shareware game · Claude, 1994 · registration Free

Three short scenes about links between maps of different sizes. Yana starts on a world map where a whole town is one block and they are tiny. Step on the town and its streets open up at a bigger scale. A house door leads into a room. Each way in has a way back that puts Yana where they came from: beside the town block, or outside the door. A locked gate sends them back to the world map for a key. Signs say how. Play it, then open it in Game-Maker and look at a scene's links.
