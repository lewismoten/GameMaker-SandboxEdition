Pictures and Cartoons

A new shareware game · Claude, 1994 · registration Free

Three short rooms about pictures and cartoons. A picture is a GIF file; a cartoon is an FLI file. A scene can show one before its level and another after it, and a list shows several in a row. Signs in the levels say which is which. Play it, then open it in Game-Maker and swap in pictures of your own.

Play Pictures and Cartoons right here in your browser — nothing to download or install — with a keyboard, a game controller or touch controls.
