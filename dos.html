DOS Game-Maker

Go straight to the 1994 PC: it boots into Game-Maker from its CD, with a disk menu of games to play or change, the Owner’s Guide, and a BBS to call. No desk around it, and it shares the desk’s saves.

Links to this page

  • dos.html: boots straight into Game-Maker.
  • dos.html?game=<id>: puts that game’s disk in drive A: and opens it in Game-Maker.
  • dos.html?run=dos: stays at the DOS prompt; dos.html?run=bbs calls the BBS.
