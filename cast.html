The cast

Teddy

Pembroke Welsh Corgi, red-gold, knows left from right

A red-gold corgi with a white blaze, big upright ears, a fluffy white rump and a grin that takes up most of his face. He knows left from right, rings the bell, balances a treat on his nose, and will happily star in any game that has a snack in it. In his own adventures he fetches Doc Leo’s scattered costume before the Big Show and sniffs his way to the county fair; elsewhere he naps, supervises and follows you round the kitchen in case anything falls.

Seen in

  • Teddy's Little Big Adventure: the hero, fetching Doc Leo’s costume
  • Teddy's Little Bigger Adventure: the hero, on Gwinn’s trail to the fair
  • Teddy's Little Biggest Adventure: the birthday boy, running errands
  • Sandcastle Knight: the corgi knight, rebuilding the castles
  • Quest for the Round Tuit: a royal hound
  • KEEP IT BRIEF! The Tiny Pockets Library: napping on a sweater in Lost and Found
  • Depth Perception: ray-traced, in chrome
  • Virus Blaster: a framed photo on the desk

  • Cleanse Me, O Loofah, But Leave My Soul: asleep in the hall
  • The Great Dairy Reckoning of 3:17 A.M.: asleep, then pacing
  • Where's the Remote?!: following you round the kitchen
  • Veni, Vidi, Risi!: a good Roman dog (CAVE CANEM)
  • Shakespeare in Sweatpants: asleep in the audience, awake for the bow
  • Tune-Up: rides shotgun in THE CHEZ and plays the brake lamps

Kit

The Starters’ first explorer, in a teal beanie

A small, cheerful explorer in a teal knit beanie with a white pom-pom, an orange hoodie with a pocket and drawstrings, gray pants and white sneakers. Kit walks, jumps, falls, climbs ladders (seen from behind), digs with a little shovel, tosses a star and cheers when a level is done. Seen from above, Kit walks in four directions. A bump only hurts a little. Kit leads the first Starter of every group and his own three; eight helpers in other colors lead the rest, in the same blocks, so what you learn from one carries to the next.

Seen in

  • Switches: pulls a lever to build a bridge
  • Scroll or Stay: stays in one room that is a whole screen, then walks a long level as the picture follows
  • Kit Ducks: ducks under a bluebird that flies at head height, then hops a rolling ball that a duck would not dodge

  • Road Tiles: follows a road of straight pieces, round corners, diagonals and a bridge, one block at a time
  • Everybody Talks: talks with Pip, and then joins a group of friends who all talk at once

Gwinn

Teddy’s big sister and sister-in-mischief

Chocolate-brown, amber-eyed, pink collar, adopted, and usually the one causing the trouble: she is the reason most of these games have a plot. She scattered the costume, flattened the sandcastles with her zoomies and chewed a slipper. She knows one trick (zoomies) and a SIT she has not quite learned. She is not allowed in the library, and she is in the library.

Doc Leo

Hobo clown, PhD in Comedic Science

Lewis’s friendly hobo clown: a chewed-up fedora, wire glasses, white greasepaint round his eyes and mouth, his own nose and cheeks sunburned pink, painted gray stubble, a purple shirt, a green tie, a corduroy jacket, blue suspenders, red sneakers and a rubber chicken on a dog leash. A hobo takes a job if one’s offered (a tramp won’t), so he hosts open mics, juggles three rubber chickens (one real), tickles rats with a certificate to prove it, and always orders root beer.

THE CHEZ

A tomato-red 1976½ CitiCar. Always charging.

Lewis’s own electric car: a tomato-red 1976½ CitiCar, about half the length of anything parked next to it, with a black roof and racing stripes (they make the car go faster). It pulls a parade float at its usual few miles an hour, clears the road with an ah-OOO-gah, and turns up at a curb in nearly every Sandbox game. It is charging. It is always charging.

The tin heart

Small, riveted, ticking. Hidden somewhere.

A little riveted tin heart that ticks, a nod to the Tin Man, who went all the way to Oz to get one, and to Lewis, whose own heart has kept time with a pacemaker since February 2014, the year Game-Maker’s source code was set free. One is hidden in every Sandbox and Bard of Bizarre game, and one more keeps time somewhere in the 1994 room, so it is something to look for: listen for the tick-tock.

Watt

Pocket-sized repair robot (there’s no place like Ohm)

Lewis “Lewie” Moten III

The person behind all of them

Teddy and Gwinn’s person, THE CHEZ’s driver, the man under Doc Leo’s fedora and the Bard behind the songs. He drew 3D art for BBSes in 1995 and has been making things for screens ever since. In the games he mostly stays behind the camera, except for one selfie with Teddy, waiting on a secret BBS.

Carl

A fly. Just a fly. (He would disagree.)

Big red eyes, a smug grin and a barrel roll that makes every swatter miss. Some say flies live twenty-four hours; Carl has broken time, and he keeps buzzing into other people’s songs: the bath, the fridge at 3:17 A.M., a haunted house and an open-mic night, where a hello is a tip.

Seen in
