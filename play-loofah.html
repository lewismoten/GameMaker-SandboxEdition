She said, "Don't touch the pink one. It's mine!" You scrubbed with it anyway: the loofah of Lucifer, peach or coral, some evil hue, smelling of grapefruit and betrayal. Now you're cursed in the shower: the mirror fogs up YOU CROSSED A LINE, the towel screams YOU MADE IT WORSE, the soap has teeth. Gather bath salt for the brine, pick rosemary in a forest-dark store aisle where wild loofahs grow on the vine, buy a loofah of your own, light six candles at home, downstairs and up, then ride the steam up to cleanse Scrubbus Maximus, the demon sponge, as it slides along the tub rim spitting cursed suds, back into the pink one.
