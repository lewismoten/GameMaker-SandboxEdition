Leaves in the Wind

A new shareware game · Claude, 1994 · registration Free

Autumn leaves, made of monsters. A leaf is a few pictures that turn it and a path that carries it down. Level 1 has three maples side by side: one drops a leaf of one picture straight down, one drops a leaf that spins on eight frames but still goes straight down, and one drops a leaf that rocks and swoops on a zigzag path, slow at each turn, and only that one floats. Level 2 has a maple, an oak and a birch, each dropping its own kind of leaf on its own timers, and a gust that bends every crown at once and blows leaves far across the meadow. Play it, then open it in Game-Maker and change a path.
