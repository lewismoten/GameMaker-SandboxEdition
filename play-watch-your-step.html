Watch Your Step

A new shareware game · Claude, 1994 · registration Free

Three short levels about things that hurt. Spikes cost a few hit points, and only once. A pool in the ground costs a whole life, and the room starts again where you came in. The third level has a safe way past both. Signs say how. Play it, then open it in Game-Maker and change a hazard.

Play Watch Your Step right here in your browser — nothing to download or install — with a keyboard, a game controller or touch controls.
