Out Loud

A new shareware game · Claude, 1994 · registration Free

Three short levels about a game that talks. A voice is a sound file: a key's move can play one, and so can a counter going up or down. Only one voice plays at a time, and a new scene cuts it off, so a speaker stands far enough from the door for the whole line. Signs say how. Play it, then open it in Game-Maker and look at the Sounds tab.

Play Out Loud right here in your browser — nothing to download or install — with a keyboard, a game controller or touch controls.
