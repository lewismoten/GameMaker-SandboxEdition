Three Phases

A new shareware game · Claude, 1994 · registration Free

Two short levels about a boss that changes as it is hurt. Big Bug paces a long tunnel and a star does not stop it. Hit it twice and it turns into a quicker orange bug, which is a different monster with its own picture and path. In the second level the last hit brings a tiny third one. Signs in the levels say how. Play it, then open it in Game-Maker and change when it changes.

Play Three Phases right here in your browser — nothing to download or install — with a keyboard, a game controller or touch controls.
