Overhead

A new shareware game · Claude, 1994 · registration Free

Two short levels about maps seen from above. In a room, gravity is off, Lami walks any of four ways and faces the way they go, and the walls and furniture you can see are what stop them. Then four small areas make an overworld: each road runs off the edge of one area and into the next, and a house has a door into a shop. Signs say how, and a page says what an overhead map cannot do. Play it, then open it in Game-Maker and look at a scene's links.
