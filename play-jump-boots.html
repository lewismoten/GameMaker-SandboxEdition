Boots and Jet Pack

A new shareware game · Claude, 1994 · registration Free

Two short levels about jumping again in the air. Nova's jump never runs out in the first, so a tap in the air lifts them again and they can tap their way up a cliff and across a gap, as if they wore a jet pack. In the second their boots hold only two jumps: each tap uses one, a third does nothing, and blue bubbles fill the boots up. Nothing here hurts. Signs explain each idea, and every level has a page on how it is made.
