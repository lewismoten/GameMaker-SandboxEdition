Teach, Test, Twist

A new shareware game · Claude, 1994 · registration Free

Three tiny levels that show how a level is designed. One idea, a gap in the ground, comes three times: first it is taught where nothing can hurt you, then it is tested with a little danger, then it is twisted into something new. Signs name each step, and the manual shows how each level is made. Play it, then use the pattern in your own levels.

Play Teach, Test, Twist right here in your browser — nothing to download or install — with a keyboard, a game controller or touch controls.
