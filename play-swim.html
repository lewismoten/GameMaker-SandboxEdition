Under the Surface

A new shareware game · Claude, 1994 · registration Free

Three short levels about water. In a creek the water pulls you to the left, in a deep pool it holds you up so you float, and in a tall pool it lifts you up and to the left to a ledge. Water is not a special thing: it is a block with a gravity setting, and the setting says which ways it pulls. Nothing here hurts. Signs explain each idea, and every level has a page on how it is made.
