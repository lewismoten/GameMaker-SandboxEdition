Little Shadows

A new shareware game · Claude, 1994 · registration Free

Shadows under things, painted with blocks. A game has no see-through colors, so a shadow is pixels drawn into the picture of whatever casts it. Level 1 is a yard of sand, grass and dark soil, played twice: once with a solid shadow under a walking pip, once with a dithered one, a checkerboard that lets the ground show through. Level 2 is three pips hopping: no shadow, a shadow that never changes, and one that shrinks at the top of the hop. Level 3 is a bee that is two blocks tall, the bee above and its shadow in a block of its own below. Play it, then open it in Game-Maker and look at the frames.

Play Little Shadows right here in your browser — nothing to download or install — with a keyboard, a game controller or touch controls.
