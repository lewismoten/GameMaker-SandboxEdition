Watt rolls out of the last
circuit board and into his very
own charging socket.

Bzzzt... ahhh.

Every appliance in the house
hums the right tune again. The
toaster toasts. The garage door
stays put. The clock radio
plays soft jazz, at a sensible
hour.




Mrs. Bitmore finds a tiny robot
asleep in the fuse box,
smiling, his little antenna
glowing green.

"Huh," she says, and closes the
panel very gently.

THE END
There's no place like Ohm.
