OHM SWEET OHM
Version 1.0 - Shareware
(c) 1995 Sandbox Software

A logic puzzle game for IBM PC
compatibles with VGA. Sound
Blaster recommended.

TO PLAY
Type OHMSWEET at the DOS prompt
(it runs PLAYGAME with
OHMSWEET.GAM).

WHAT IS IT?
Watt, a pocket-sized repair
robot, must roll through
fifteen haywire circuit boards
to get home to his charging
socket. Flip switches, blow
fuses, throw relays and route
live current to shut down the
force-field gates... without
touching a live wire yourself.
