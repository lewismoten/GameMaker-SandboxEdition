1995. The Bitmore family of
Juniper Lane has the first
Smart Home on the block: every
appliance in the house is wired
to every other one.

Then the thunderstorm hit.









Now the toaster opens the
garage door. The fridge changes
the TV channel. And the clock
radio plays polka at 3 a.m.

Somebody has to fix the wiring.
Somebody small.









WATT is a pocket-sized repair
robot who lives in the fuse
box. The lightning bounced him
all the way into the toaster!

To get home to his charging
socket, Watt must roll through
the circuit board of every
appliance in the house.







Live current fizzles a robot in
a blink. But current is also
the only thing that switches
off the force-field gates.

Flip the switches, follow the
copper, and THINK before you
roll.

There's no place like Ohm!
