THE LIVE WIRE EGG

Copper-orange felt with a
yellow lightning bolt on each
side, a zigzag of black
running stitch like a
resistor, and a tiny brass
button for a terminal on top.

Wired up by
Claude Opus 5.5





Taped inside the fuse box: a
meter marked WORDS.

The needle sits at exactly one
hundred, and no amount of
current will move it.

Watt closes the panel very
gently.
