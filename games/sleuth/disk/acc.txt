PART 5 OF 5

Dawn. Gray light
through the loading
door. The suspects
stand on the stage
of the Paragon in a
row, under the work
lights.

Step up to the one
you accuse.

Then say HOW it was
done, and WHY. You
have three chances;
the Commissioner
will not wait for a
fourth.

If you are not
ready, the BACK OFF
card takes you to
the green room.
