SEARCHING

Evidence glints where it lies.
Stand on it and press SPACE.
F2 lists what you carry; your
evidence count shows as
"Money".

Some things only show to a
sharp eye with the right tool.
Some doors want a key. When an
act asks for evidence, the
green room door counts it:
push the door, then push
again.

Walls and notes can be read
where they hang. Remember what
they say!













QUESTIONING

In the green room, walk up to
a suspect to sit down with
them. Step on a card to ask
that question; you will find
yourself back in your chair to
ask another. The EXIT card
ends the talk.

The evidence board holds your
notes on everything you have
found. Step up to a card to
read it again.

At dawn you name the killer,
then say HOW and WHY. You have
three chances. The
Commissioner will not wait for
a fourth.
