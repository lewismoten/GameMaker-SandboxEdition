FINAL CURTAIN
An Inspector Ada Penn Mystery
Version 1.0 - 1995
(c) 1995 Sandbox Software

Port Ellery, Halloween night,
1936. Opening night of THE
RAVEN'S BRIDE at the Paragon
Theatre. In Act III the heroine
stabs the villain with a trick
dagger, and the villain -
Victor Crane, the Paragon's
owner and star - falls down
dead. The dagger was real.

You are Inspector Ada Penn of
Homicide, and you have until
dawn. Search the empty theater
with the house lights up, then
again at 2 AM with only the
ghost light burning. Question
the leading lady, the
understudy, the widow, the
stage manager and the critic.
Then gather them on the stage
and name the killer - and say
how, and why.

STARTING THE GAME
  Type FINAL and press Enter. A
  VGA display is required. A
  Sound Blaster (or compatible)
  is recommended for the music
  and the digitized sound
  effects; the PC speaker works
  too.

KEYS
  Arrows or W A S D: walk
  Q / E: walk up-left /
  up-right
  Number pad 7 9 1 3: diagonals
  Space: pick up evidence
  Z: look through the lens
  F1 help, F2 evidence, F3
  music, F4 sound, F5 save, F6
  load, Esc quit
