THE VELVET CURTAIN
EGG

Between the green
room's sofa
cushions: a felt
egg, deep theater
red, with gold
checks, a comedy
mask and a gold
curtain-tie.

PERFORMED BY
Doc Leo, hobo clown
(standing backward
in line, as usual)
A NOTE TUCKED INSIDE

The case notes say
every word fell out
of every book in
town on the same
night.

The last line,
underlined twice:

"Check the little
library with the red
door."
