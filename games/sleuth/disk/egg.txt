THE VELVET CURTAIN
EGG

Between the green
room's sofa
cushions: a felt
egg, deep theater
red, with gold
checks, a comedy
mask and a gold
curtain-tie.

PERFORMED BY
Doc Leo, hobo clown
(standing backward
in line, as usual)
A NOTE TUCKED INSIDE

Case 13, never
closed: one recipe
card lifted in 1776,
one root beer the
family will not
discuss, one kitchen
door with flour on
the knob.

Last note,
underlined twice:
"Count the eggs
again. A baker
always does."
