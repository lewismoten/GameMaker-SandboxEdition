IN THE YEAR 2000!
A Future Adventure
Sandbox Software, (C) 1993
Version 1.1
==============================

It's the year 2000! Cars fly.
Robots mow the lawn. Dinner
comes in a pill. The aliens
next door are VERY friendly.
And there's a phone booth on
every single corner, because
every phone booth is a
Teleport-A-Phone now - only 25
cents a call.

Dex Dynamo, age 11, wrote the
greatest science fair report
in the solar system: "THE
POCKET TELEPHONE." Then Sparky
3000, his robot dog, ate it.
The Science Fair is today, at
3:00. On the Moon.

Help Dex find his pages in
seven levels of the future:

1. DOME SWEET DOME: the
   suburbs of Hover Heights
2. THE CLOUD LANE: Grandpa's
   flying CitiCar, nine miles
   to the Skyway
3. THE INFORMATION
   SUPERHIGHWAY: fourteen
   lanes of hover traffic
4. MEGA MALL 2000: the arcade,
   TV-RAMA, and the food court
5. THE TELEPORT-A-PHONE
   EXCHANGE: wrong number!
   (several of them)
6. THE MOON: moon buggies and
   meteor showers
7. THE SCIENCE FAIR: catch
   Sparky before the judges go

REQUIREMENTS
------------
* 286 or better, VGA graphics,
  640K of memory, a hard disk.
* Sound Blaster (or
  compatible) for music and
  digitized sound; the PC
  speaker works too.

To play, copy all the files to
one directory and type
YEAR2000.

CONTROLS
--------
* Arrow keys: walk (joystick
  works too); in Grandpa's
  CitiCar, Left and Right
  steer
* W A S D: walk, same as the
  arrows
* Q / E: walk up-left /
  up-right
* Keypad 7 9 1 3: walk
  diagonally
* Space: fire the Laser-Tag
  Zapper
* F1 help, F2 status
  (quarters!)
* F3 music, F4 sound on/off
* F5 save, F6 load, Esc quit

HINTS
-----
* Phone booths cost a quarter.
  Keep one in your pocket!
* Wait for the laser fences to
  blink off.
* Zapping a car only makes it
  swerve. Save your charge for
  robots.
* In the Teleport-A-Phone
  Exchange, booth number 4 is
  out of order. Try another
  number - you'll get there
  eventually.
* Read the teletype in the
  exchange hall. It knows a
  thing or two.
* Grandpa lost his key ring in
  the hedge garden. His
  CitiCar won't open without
  it.
* Grandpa's CitiCar likes a
  honk.
* In the Cloud Lane, traffic
  starts as a dot on the
  horizon. Pick a clear lane
  early, and keep right when
  the sign nears 0 MI.
* The judges want 20 pages.
  There are lots more than
  that.
* Came to the fair short? The
  school copier prints more.
