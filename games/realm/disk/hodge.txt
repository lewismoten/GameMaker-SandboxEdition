Procrastislimes got into the
turnips again. They'll attack
you later. Later's usually
when you least expect it.

Whack 'em with your sword.
They drop gems, and gems are
as good as gold in any shop in
the kingdom.

Bats come out up by the hills.
You'll want a better sword
than that stick for the bigger
trouble. The smith in
Laterburg sells steel.

The lad with the shovel at the
end of the turnips is Lewie.
Hardest worker in Nowish,
which isn't saying much. He's
going to make games of his own
one day, he says, with
something called a Game-Maker.
In Procrastia, "one day" is a
big promise. He means it.
