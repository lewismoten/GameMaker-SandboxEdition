The stone slides aside. Under
it, a note in a tidy hand:

THE HERALDRY EGG. Forest-green
felt quartered in gold
diamonds like a herald's coat,
a silver felt sword laid over
a scrap of treasure map, and a
road of brown backstitch
winding all the way round the
shell.

Stitched inside the lining:



Questmaster Gwinn (two n's,
three quests, never once asked
for directions)

Map inked by Lewis "Lewie"
Moten III

Roads curved by Claude Opus
5.5







The royal map ends at the edge
of the kingdom with "Here Be
Root Beer," and a line the
cartographer was far too
polite to finish: "Taken from
the Redcoats, fair and square,
on a Tuesday, by..."
