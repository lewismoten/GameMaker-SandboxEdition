A hero! Splendid. We've been
meaning to send for one ever
since the Tuit went missing.

The Royal Round Tuit, boy!
Every King of Procrastia has
got around to things by it.
Then Lord Deadline stole it
and broke it in three, and now
nobody gets around to
ANYTHING.





The first piece is under the
Snooze Barrows, north of here.
Take this ROYAL WRIT: the
Barrows' door is sealed in my
name.

(You may already have one.
Have another. They're free.)

Bring the pieces to Deadline
Keep, beyond the Dawdle Flats.
And do come back and tell me
how it went. Whenever.
