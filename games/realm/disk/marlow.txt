Fog's been sitting on the
Soggy Vaults for years. Old
sunken temple at the end of
the long pier.

Only my foghorn shifts it, and
I lent it to... somebody in
the Snooze Barrows. Probably
snoring on it.

Find the foghorn, then walk
into the fog bank on the pier.
One honk and it'll clear right
off.


Mind the Tide Crabs in there.
Steel, lad. Steel.
