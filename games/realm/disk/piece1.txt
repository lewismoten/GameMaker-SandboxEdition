PIECE 1 OF 3
Dungeon 1 of 4 done

Sir Snoozalot rolls over,
mumbles "five more minutes,"
and is gone in a puff of dust.

Behind him, on a little stone
plinth: a wedge of old oak
edged in brass. The first
piece of the Royal Round Tuit!

It's warm, and it hums
faintly, like something that
very much wants to get on with
things.
(It glows on the Tuit Table in
the Garden Court now. Two to
go, then Deadline Keep. Time
to save: F5.)
