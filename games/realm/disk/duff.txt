The Putoff Mines are closed
until further notice.

Further notice: you can go in.
Up the road north, in the
mountain.

It's black as a pocket in
there. Buy a lamp at Hattie's
stall or you'll get nowhere:
the door won't let you past
without one.




Something's been ticking down
there for weeks. Tick, tock.
Tick, tock. Like it's waiting
for something.
