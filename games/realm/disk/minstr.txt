Kazoo reeds! Ten gold a
bundle, ten notes a bundle.

No kazoo yet? Doc Leo teaches
the Kazoo of Haste, if you do
him a favor. Until then you
can hum through the reeds and
press K. People will stare.

Tonics from Mother Tansy's
cousin at the next stall,
fifteen gold.




The bald fellow in the ruff
and the sweatpants, down the
end of the quay? That's Will,
a playwright, visiting. Eleven
years on one play. He has the
title. ALAS, he says.
FORSOOTH.

He swears there's a little tin
heart in the grass by the
lighthouse, ticking away.
"Found it! Ticks like it means
it," he keeps saying. He
hasn't gone to look.
