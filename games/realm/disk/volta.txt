Behold: THE CHEZ! A horseless
carriage from the far-off
future of 1976 and a half!

She runs on lightning. Her
charge port is on the rear
passenger side, for reasons I
have never got around to
understanding.







She'll cross the Dawdle Flats,
forty leagues of tidal mud
nobody has ever finished
walking. But she's flat. Bring
me anything with lightning in
it and I'll open the garage
door.

They say there's lightning in
the Soggy Vaults. Charging
takes one mile per hour. Per
hour.




Arrows drive her. SPACE
squeezes the horn: ah-OOO-gah!
Crabs can't stand it. And when
the low causeway goes puddly,
the tide's coming in: wait for
it to drain.
