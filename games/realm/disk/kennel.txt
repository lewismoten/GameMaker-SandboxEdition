The Royal Hounds ran off
again!

TEDDY is a corgi. Red and
white, ears like two sails,
and he knows his left from his
right. Last seen in the
Whistling Woods east of the
river.

GWINN is chocolate brown, a
pit and lab, pink collar. Two
n's in Gwinn. She's particular
about that. She likes to dig
on the beach south of Someday
Harbor.
Find them. They'll come home
on their own once they're
found. Dogs know the way, they
just don't feel like it. Then
come to the kennel gate.
