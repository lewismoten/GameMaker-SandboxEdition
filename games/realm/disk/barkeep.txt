Welcome to the Procrastinating
Pony! A bed's ten gold at the
counter: sleep it off, wake up
whole.

Root beer? Family recipe. A
dozen ingredients and a
thirteenth nobody will name.
Gran swore it's why we won a
war once. Nobody talks about
it.

Eventually Falls who says the
falls hide a garden, the
garden hides a nest, and the
nest is only full some of the
time. Hermits.
