The Kraken of Kinda-Later
sinks back into the deep,
promising to come back for you
eventually.

The second piece of the Round
Tuit sits in a scallop shell,
crackling with blue sparks.
Lightning! It must have soaked
it up from the storm-eels.

Professor Fiddlesticks wanted
anything with lightning in it.
THE CHEZ will drink this right
up. (Don't worry. It's a tuit.
It recharges.)
