Tick-Tock the Unpunctual winds
down, chimes thirteen, and
stops for good. Fashionably
late to its own defeat.

The third piece of the Round
Tuit was the key that wound it
up.

Three pieces. One Round Tuit.
All that's left is to get
around to Deadline Keep, south
of Eventually Falls, past the
Gloomwood.


A night at the Falls first,
maybe. They say Lord Deadline
fights twice: once in his
armor, and once without it.
