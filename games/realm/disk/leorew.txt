REGGIE! You found him! Sir
Reginald Cluckington III, home
at last! Squeak!

As promised: the Kazoo of
Haste. Press Z to toot. Every
toot is a note that flies
straight and true, and a sharp
note beats anything short of
the King.

Twenty-five notes to start.
Lyra sells reeds at the
harbor, ten notes a bundle.


Now if you'll excuse me, I'm
going to stand backward in a
line.
