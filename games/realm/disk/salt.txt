Fish ain't biting. They're
putting it off.

Seen a brown dog digging on
the beach south of here, by
the rocks. Pink collar. Dug up
my lunch, my boot and a felt
hat I never owned.

East of the Flats there's
Eventually Falls, the Putoff
Mines and that black castle
with the stopped clock. Nobody
goes there. Nobody's got round
to it.
