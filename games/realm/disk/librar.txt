Shh. The Tome of Tomorrow is
three hundred years overdue.

The fine is nine thousand
gold. I would settle for the
book.

It was last borrowed by a
miner from Eventually Falls,
and it has not been seen since
they closed the Putoff Mines.
The mines are as dark as a
closed book. Take a lamp.



Bring it to the library gate.
We are open Monday through
Someday.
