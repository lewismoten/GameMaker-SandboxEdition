You found my garden! Most folk
walk right past a waterfall.
Never think to walk through
one.

The nest? Something made of
felt and gold turns up in it
now and again, then it's gone.
Watch it a while.

When it's there, stand over it
and press ENTER. You'll keep
it in your satchel. F2 shows
you.


And if you have it, the stone
by the nest has more to say.
