MAXIMUS: THE HUNDRED WORLDS - HOW FAR GAME-MAKER GOES
======================================================

Maximus was built to find the edges of Game-Maker 3.0 and to
stand on them. These are the limits we found in its file
formats and in PLAYGAME, and how close this game comes.

SCENES IN A .GAM
  Limit: 231 (PLAYGAME allocates all scenes in one 64K block of 283-byte records)
  Used:  100: title, ending, training deck, galaxy, 5 sectors, 88 worlds, Maelstrom, Citadel, Heart. We stopped at 100: 28300 bytes of scene records sit easily in DOS memory, every scene still loads in a moment, and the integrator's 310x165 world view still shows each scene box and link
  Where: MAXIMUS.GAM

SCENE FILE-NAME POOL
  Limit: 65,520 bytes (PLAYGAME clamps it at 0xFFF0)
  Used:  2903 bytes
  Where: MAXIMUS.GAM

LINKS PER SCENE
  Limit: 20
  Used:  20 (a sector chart: the warp home, 18 planets and the jump ring onward)
  Where: SECTOR1..5

FILES PER SCENE
  Limit: 20
  Used:  12 (all 12 slots PLAYGAME reads for the title scene, all 11 for every world, all 4 for the ending)
  Where: all scenes

MAP SIZE
  Limit: 100 x 100 blocks
  Used:  98 maps, every one 100 x 100 and wrapping all the way round
  Where: *.MAP

MONSTERS PLACED ON A MAP
  Limit: 200
  Used:  200 in the Maelstrom (every slot: not one left for a shot); 7319 in all
  Where: MAEL.MAP

MONSTERS IN A .MON
  Limit: 64
  Used:  64 in each of 8 files (512 monsters, 352 creatures + the courier's own shots and loot)
  Where: *.MON

ANIMATION STEPS PER MONSTER
  Limit: 21 slots (PLAYGAME steps through 20)
  Used:  all 20 in every creature: up to 3 drawn frames cycling with varied timing
  Where: *.MON

PATH CORNERS PER MONSTER
  Limit: 10 (x 2 paths)
  Used:  10 (hopping slimes, figure eights)
  Where: *.MON

MONSTER BLOCKS IN A .MBL
  Limit: 100
  Used:  100 in each of 4 files
  Where: LAND/SEA/SPACE/MECH.MBL

BACKGROUND BLOCKS IN A .BBL
  Limit: 150
  Used:  150 in each of 4 files (600 blocks)
  Where: SURFACE/OCEAN/STATION/SPACE.BBL

CHARACTER BLOCKS IN A .CBL
  Limit: 50
  Used:  50 in each of 4 files
  Where: COURIER/POD/DIVER/ROVER.CBL

CHARACTER SEQUENCES
  Limit: 20 x 10 frames
  Used:  all 20 in each of 4 .CHR files; the Nova burst uses all 10 frames
  Where: *.CHR

INVENTORY SLOTS
  Limit: 10
  Used:  10 courier modules
  Where: module blocks 140..149

COUNTERS (METERS)
  Limit: 30
  Used:  hit points, lives, credits, 15 ammo counters, all 10 extra counters (the star-shards)
  Where: counters 20..29

SOUND EFFECTS IN A .SND
  Limit: 30
  Used:  30, each with its own digitized .VOC (30 files)
  Where: MAXIMUS.SND

PALETTE COLORS
  Limit: 256 (255 drawable; 255 is the sprite clear color)
  Used:  86 shared + 149 per place = 235; 98 palettes, one per scene
  Where: *.PAL

MONSTERS DRAWN AT ONCE
  Limit: 50
  Used:  Maelstrom keeps under it so none goes invisible
  Where: MAEL.MAP

DEMO RECORDING
  Limit: 1000 key events
  Used:  796 events
  Where: DEMO.REC

TEXT SCREEN WIDTH
  Limit: 31 columns
  Used:  30 (a full 31 leaves its newline behind: a blank line)
  Where: INSTRUCT.TXT

CMF INSTRUMENTS / FM VOICES
  Limit: 16 / 9 (6 + 5 drums in rhythm mode)
  Used:  15 songs, rhythm mode in all
  Where: *.CMF

PICTURES BETWEEN SCENES
  Limit: any GIF, FLI, BKD, TXT or LST
  Used:  23 GIFs, 5 FLIs, 179 BKD windows into 6 big GIFs (each holds a sector's planet cards and scans side by side), 93 slide shows
  Where: CARDS1..5.GIF, PLACES.GIF

Some limits shaped the game:

 * Game-Maker animates block TYPES on one clock, so every
   force field of a world blinks together. Vending pads
   cannot restock without restocking all at once, so each
   sells one of each per visit.
 * A scene reloads its map each time you enter, so star-
   shards are counters (20..29), not objects: a shard cannot
   be counted twice and gates test for it without using it.
 * The Maelstrom fills all 200 monster slots, so there is no
   slot left for a single shot: in the storm, weapons jam.
 * Every map wraps, so every planet is a little globe: walk
   east long enough and you come home.
 * One big GIF per sector holds every planet card and every
   long-range scan; each .BKD file shows its own 320x200
   window of it, so 176 pictures load from 5 files.
