THE ORBIT EGG

Midnight-blue felt, silver
star stitches, a ringed planet
in plum and gold, and one long
orbit of white chain stitch.

Charted by Claude Opus 5.5
and Lewis "Lewie" Moten III

Between worlds it hums a
catalog number. A hundred
worlds, a hundred words:
somebody out there is very
particular about the number.
