HOW TO PLAY MAXIMUS

Find the ten star-shards and
carry their light to the Core.

THE GALAXY
You fly the courier pod. Thrust
with the arrow keys. Fly into a
warp ring to jump to a sector.
Gates of colored crystal bar
the outer sectors: touch a gate
while you carry its shard and
it opens.

SECTOR CHARTS
Fly into the middle of a planet
to land. Planets with a gold
signal beside them hold a
shard. Trading posts sell
supplies: nudge a pad to buy,
one of each per visit.

ON FOOT
Left/Right walk. Up fires your
jets: tap it again in the air
to climb higher. Down curls
into a ball. Space fires your
blaster the way you last moved.
P picks things up. To go back
to orbit, walk into the uplink
beacon.

UNDER THE SEA
The diving bell swims with the
arrows and coasts when you let
go. Surface at the buoy to
leave.

ROVER WORLDS
The rover drives fast and rides
the lifts. With Spring Shocks,
Up makes it jump.

MODULES
Ten modules hide in planet
vaults. Each adds a key:
Z Plasma Lance (kills armor)
X Nova Core (8-way burst)
Q/E Dash Boots
C Shield Emitter (guard orb)
F Hover Pack
V Mine Layer
H Med Kit
W Flak Cannon
Q/E Hydro Jets (diving bell)
Up Spring Shocks (rover)
T Hail (say hello)

VAULTS
Credit doors cost 10 credits to
open. Force fields blink on and
off. Crystal walls shatter at a
touch.

KEYS
F1 help F2 status
F3 music F4 sound
F5 save F6 load
Esc quit

Good luck, courier.
