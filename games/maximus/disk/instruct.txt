HOW TO PLAY MAXIMUS

Find the ten star-shards and
carry their light to the Core.

THE GALAXY
You fly the courier pod.
Thrust with the arrow keys.
Fly into a warp ring to jump
to a sector. Gates of colored
crystal bar the outer sectors:
touch a gate while you carry
its shard and it opens.

SECTOR CHARTS
Fly into the middle of a
planet to land. Planets with a
gold signal beside them hold a
shard. The planet card shows a
long-range scan: green is you,
gold is the vault. Trading
posts sell supplies: nudge a
pad to buy, one of each per
visit. A jump ring behind
gates takes you on to the next
sector.

ON FOOT
Left/Right walk (the number
pad's 4/6/8/2 work as arrows
too). Up fires your jets: tap
it again in the air to climb
higher. Down curls into a
ball. Space fires your blaster
the way you last moved. P
picks things up and O drops
one. To go back to orbit, walk
into the uplink beacon.

UNDER THE SEA
The diving bell swims with the
arrows and holds still when
you let go. Surface at the
buoy to leave.

ROVER WORLDS
The rover drives fast and
rides the lifts. With Spring
Shocks, Up makes it jump.

MODULES
Ten modules hide in planet
vaults. Each adds a key:
Z Plasma Lance (kills armor)
X Nova Core (8-way burst)
Q/E Dash Boots
C Shield Emitter (guard orb)
F Hover Pack
V Mine Layer
R Med Kit
W Flak Cannon
Q/E Hydro Jets (diving bell)
Up Spring Shocks (rover)
T Hail (say hello)

VAULTS
Credit doors cost 10 credits
to open. Force fields blink on
and off. Crystal walls shatter
at a touch. A healing spring
in the caves mends 100 hit
points in one drink, then runs
dry until you land again.

THE HEART
A seal of light bars the way
out. Crack the Heart Eye three
times (plasma, a shield orb,
or ramming it, which hurts)
and its light will come to you
and open the seal.

KEYS
F1 help F2 status
F3 music F4 sound
F5 save F6 load
Esc quit

Good luck, courier.
