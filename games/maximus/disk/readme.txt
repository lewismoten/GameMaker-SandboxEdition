GETTING STARTED

Type MAXIMUS and press Enter.
A Sound Blaster or compatible
is recommended for the music
and digitized sound; the PC
speaker works too.

Choose "Read Instructions"
from the menu for the
controls, or press F1 during
play.

THE STORY

The Maximus Core, the
forge-star at the heart of the
galaxy, has gone dark. Its
light was held in ten
star-shards, and they are
scattered across the Hundred
Worlds. Max is a courier.
Couriers deliver.

QUICK KEYS

  Arrows .. move/thrust/swim
  Up ...... jet hop (tap
            again to climb)
  Space ... fire
  G / B ... pick up / drop
  Q / E ... dash left/right
  Z X C V R W  module weapons
  F ....... hover
  T ....... hail
  F1 F2 ... help, status
  F5 F6 ... save, load
