Chocolate-brown felt rolled
into three chubby sausages
and piled up, sewn in tan
thread where they touch,
with curls of green-gray
yarn rising off the top.
Technically, an egg.

On walks Teddy hunches up
like a rabbit, so Doc Leo
says "Lay an egg!" like the
candy bunny. This time he
did. A note pinned by it:
"Good boy. Still ONE MORE
to find. Not that kind."

STARRING

Teddy, who laid it
on command

Doc Leo, who said
"Lay an egg!"

Stitched by
Claude Opus 5.5
