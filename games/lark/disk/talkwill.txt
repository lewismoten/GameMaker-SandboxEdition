"Joe! Thank heaven, a friend!"
Will clutches his bald head
with both hands. His ruff is
crooked. His slippers are on
the wrong feet.

"I am next! And my sonnet is
everywhere!"








The fans caught his pages when
he stood up. 3 are still going
round and round under them.
The rest went whirling up the
turret stair.

"The stair door sticks," says
Will. "Bring me the first
pages and it will open. It
knows."






UP THE TURRET
Gravity pulls down in there,
but warm air from the ovens
rises: walk into it and it
carries you up.

The fan's draft blows straight
across. Hop up into it and let
it carry you.







Walk into a page to take it.
There are 4 up the turret. The
back stair at the top comes
down by the stage.
