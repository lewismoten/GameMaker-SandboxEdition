Opening time at the Tiny
Pockets Library. The note is
gone from the desk, and Joe is
back behind it, reading
glasses on, bow tie straight.

He reads the tally out loud,
to nobody in particular.








"Steps: 12,816. Valentines
returned: 12. Walks shoveled:
5. Pages caught: 7. Rocks
rolled home: 4.

Hearts: 1. Gold."










"I stopped counting after
that," says Joe.

"Some things you don't count.
You just have them."

Click.
