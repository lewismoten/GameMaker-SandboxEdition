"Hello? Is somebody there?"
The voice comes out of a stone
cubby with its door shut
tight.

It is Loaf #13, the loaf
nobody picked, who rolls
wherever he likes. He rolled
in here out of the rain.







"Somebody painted a rock for
everyone who ever needed
cheering up," says Loaf. "Each
one lives in its own painted
circle."

"The rain set them all
rolling. And this door only
unlatches when every rock is
home."






THE PAINTED ROCKS
A painted rock rolls away from
Joe, along its channel of
sand, until a wall stops it.

Come at it from the far side
and it rolls into its circle.
Then give it a pat (walk into
it).







4 rocks, 4 circles. One
channel turns a corner: to
send its rock round, stand
where it would roll away from
you the right way.
