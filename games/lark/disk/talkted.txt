Teddy is at the gate, ears
straight up, both of them, the
way they go when something is
very wrong.

He tells Joe all about it in
corgi, which Joe reads
fluently.








There were 12 valentines for
the party. The wind took six
of them and blew them all over
the lawn.

Gwinn took the other six. In
the box. In her mouth. At a
run.








"She is in her yard," Teddy
says, "being very fast about
it."

Joe counts the lawn: six
valentines, all tumbling.

Gwinn runs away from whoever
comes near. Walls stop her,
though.
