"Joe! Three times in one
night!" Doc Leo is holding an
empty leash.

"I bought Sir Reginald a bunch
of balloons. That was a
mistake. He is rubber. He
weighs nothing."








Up above the carousel, Reggie
is floating to and fro under
his balloons, looking down at
everybody, the way a chicken
does.

"He's over the canopy," says
Doc Leo. "If only somebody
could get up there."







THE CAROUSEL
The horses go up and down on
their poles: the brass ones
rise while the white ones
fall.

Stand on a horse's saddle and
ride it up. Step across to the
next one when it is low.







From the last horse, the
canopy. Catch Reggie as he
floats by.

Then bring the good news to
Doc Leo, at the gate. From the
side, DOWN picks things up.
