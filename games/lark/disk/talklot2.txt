The charger hums. A little
light by THE CHEZ's back wheel
glows. Doc Leo pats her roof
like a horse.

"Listen to that motor warm
up," he says.









Joe asks his question: where
could a robot find a real
heart?

"Right there," says Doc Leo,
pointing at the motor. "That's
the heart of a car. Every
car's got one."








Joe listens. Hmm-mmm, goes the
motor, waiting for the road.

"It's THE CHEZ's heart," says
Joe. "It's working very hard.
But it's hers."

Doc Leo nods. He gives Joe
THE CHEZ's spare fuse, and a
gold thank-you heart. Joe
counts: five.
