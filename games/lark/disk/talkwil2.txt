Will reads his sonnet. All
fourteen lines. Nobody in the
Hive drinks a sip until it is
over, and then everybody
stands up.

Will bows so low his ruff
touches the floor.








Joe asks his question: where
could a robot find a real
heart?

Will taps his sleeve. "Here! I
wear my heart upon my sleeve,
Joe. Everyone can see it. It
is very embarrassing."








Joe looks at Will's sleeve.
There is a little red heart
stitched on it, crooked.

"You put your whole heart into
that sonnet," says Joe. "I
heard it. It is yours, though.
You wrote it."








Will gives Joe his umbrella
("A poet is always caught out
in the weather") and a gold
thank-you heart.

Joe counts: eight.

WILL'S UMBRELLA
Pick it up (SPACE). It keeps
the weather off.
