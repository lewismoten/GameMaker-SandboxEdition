The music box plays! Its
little dancer turns, the toys
rattle on their shelves, and
the third goat from the left
dances on her hind legs, very
badly, with all her heart.










Joe asks her what he came to
ask: where could a robot find
a real heart?

She looks at the music box. It
is playing the song it always
plays. It will play it for
ever.








"That is a memory," says Joe,
kindly. "Not a heart. A heart
would learn a new song."

The goat thinks this over.
Then she gives him the little
dancer, who came loose, and a
gold thank-you heart.

Joe counts: four.
