WALKING
Arrows, W A S D or the number
pad: one step at a time. Hold
to keep going.

Walk into a friend to talk.

SPACE: pick up what you are
standing on.







IN THE CLOCK TOWER
Gravity pulls down in there.

Arrows walk. UP hops; hold it
to hop higher. Q and E (or 7
and 9) hop up and to the side.

Wooden floors are solid on top
only: hop up through them.







TICK AND TOCK
The brass bridges and the
copper bridges take turns.
Each one blinks red before it
lets go.

Wait for yours.

Falling only takes you down a
floor. Nothing in the tower
can hurt you.





THE ROAD
Gates on the Long Road open
for the thank-yous of friends
you have helped.

F2 shows Joe's wind and his
spare bulbs. F5 saves, F6
loads, ESC stops.








KEEPSAKES
Some keepsakes teach Joe a
move, wherever gravity pulls:

SPACE: spring hop (the winding
key)
ENTER: rise (the heart
balloon)
X: HELLO (the drumstick)
DOWN: pick up, from the side
