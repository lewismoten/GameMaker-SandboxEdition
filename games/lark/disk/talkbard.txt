"Hark! My sticks!" cries the
Bard of Bizarre. The rooster
on his shoulder straightens
its spectacles.

BOOM goes the bass drum, and
the band strikes up at last.









Joe asks his question: where
could a robot find a real
heart?

"Listen!" says the Bard, and
he strikes the big drum. BOOM.
BOOM.

"There's a heart for you! The
heart of the parade!"






Joe listens carefully. BOOM.
Then nothing, until the Bard
strikes it again.

"It only beats when somebody
hits it," says Joe. "A heart
ought to beat by itself.
Shouldn't it?"

The Bard has no song about
that. Yet.





He gives Joe his spare
drumstick, with a red ribbon
on it, and a gold thank-you
heart.

Joe counts: three.

THE BARD'S DRUMSTICK
Pick it up (SPACE), for luck.
