The cubby door swings open,
and out rolls Loaf #13, warm
and a little steamy, the way a
loaf is when it is happy.

"Every rock home!" he says.
"And me with them."









Joe asks his question: where
could a robot find a real
heart?

Loaf rolls back into the cubby
and comes out with a stone:
smooth, gray, heart-shaped,
with a little sun painted on
it.







"This one was painted for me,"
says Loaf. "A heart that lasts
for ever. Stones don't break."

Joe holds it. It is cool and
smooth and very, very still.

"It does not do anything,"
says Joe. "But it was made for
you. That is something."






Loaf gives him the stone
anyway ("I have the sun") and
a gold thank-you heart.

Joe counts: nine.
