Every walk on the street is
clear, and every doormat
swept. Lewie whoops so loud
that snow slides off his roof.

"Come in and get warm, Joe!"










Inside, the fire is going.
There are mittens drying on
the hearth and a cat asleep in
front of it.

Joe asks his question: where
could a robot find a real
heart?








"Right here," says Lewie, and
nods at the fire. "Every house
has one. The hearth. It's
where everybody comes to get
warm."

Joe holds his tin hands out to
it. They warm up nicely.








"It is a good heart," says
Joe. "It belongs to the
house."

"Keep the shovel," says Lewie.
"You never know when it'll
snow." He gives Joe a gold
thank-you heart, too.

Joe counts: seven.
