"Beep," says Watt. "I fix
things, Joe. There's nothing
in here to fix."

Joe looks down. The brass
tally counter is glowing gold.
It has been counting all
along: every kindness he gave,
and every one he was given.







The whole house turns gold.
And there is a honk outside.

THE CHEZ pulls up, Doc Leo at
the wheel and Teddy in the
front passenger seat, Gwinn
running alongside, and
everybody else piling in
behind.







Everybody Joe helped came to
see.

Joe looks at them all, and at
the gold counter, and says it
out loud:

"...Huh. It was in there the
whole time."
