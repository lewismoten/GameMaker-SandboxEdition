The star shakes out its points
and sails up into the sky,
back to its place, and the
whole plant glows.

The top leaf uncurls, and
there is the mustard seed,
beaming.








Joe asks his question: where
could a robot find a real
heart?

"Up there," says the seed, and
points his little arm at the
star. "It shines for
everybody. That's a heart, I'd
say."







Joe looks at the star a long
while. It is very beautiful,
and very far away.

"It is the sky's," says Joe.
"I would not take it down. Not
even for a heart."









"Then go and see Watt," says
the seed. "The robot who fixes
things. His workshop is in the
house at the end of the road."

He gives Joe a gold thank-you
heart. Joe counts: eleven.
