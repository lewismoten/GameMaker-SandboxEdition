"Joe! Twice in one night!"
says Doc Leo. "THE CHEZ ran
out of charge, so I pushed her
the last block to the charger.
And it's off."

Sir Reginald looks tired. He
did not push.








"There are 3 breakers, one in
every part of the lot. The
gates between them open and
shut on timers: amber means
they're about to shut."

"I'd go myself, but somebody
has to keep THE CHEZ company."
