THE SMART HOME

The road ends at the Bitmores'
house, the smart one. A storm
scrambled it, and it is still
in a grump: toasters hopping,
the vacuum sulking, the
thermostat scowling.








At the back of the house, by
his charging socket, is the
workshop of Watt, the little
repair robot who can fix
anything.

Joe has carried every keepsake
all this way.








HELLO
X, with the Bard's drumstick:
a friendly BOOM thrown the way
Joe last moved. It cheers a
grumpy gadget right up.

Bumping a grumpy one winds Joe
down a little.








The thermostat is the Big
Grumble. Each HELLO that
reaches it calms it a little,
but it lets off steam first,
longer every time. Wait, then
HELLO again.

Tall boards: the winding key's
spring hop (SPACE), or the
heart balloon (ENTER).
