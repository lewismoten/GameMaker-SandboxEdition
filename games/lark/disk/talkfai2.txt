Reggie is back on his leash,
and the balloons are tied to a
fence post, all but one: a red
heart-shaped balloon.

The Clown Club cheers. One of
them honks a horn twice, which
means "hooray" in Clown.








Joe asks his question, for the
third time tonight: where
could a robot find a real
heart?

Doc Leo hands him the heart
balloon. "There's a heart,
Joe. Light as air. It lifts
you right up."







Joe holds the string. The
balloon tugs upward, hopeful.

"It is a very nice heart,"
says Joe. "But if I let go, it
would float away. A heart
ought to stay."

"Then don't let go," says Doc
Leo, and gives him a gold
thank-you heart. Joe counts:
ten.




THE HEART BALLOON
Pick it up (DOWN, from the
side). While Joe carries it,
wherever gravity pulls, hold
ENTER to rise.

It holds only so much air. Run
it out and ENTER only gets a
"hm?". Little puffs of breeze
fill it up again.
