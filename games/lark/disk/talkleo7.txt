"Seven o'clock! You did it,
Joe!"

The band starts up. The float
rolls. A clown somewhere honks
a horn on purpose.










Joe asks Doc Leo what he came
to ask: does he know where a
robot could find a real heart?

Doc Leo thinks about it.











"Some folks say a clock is a
heart," he says. "Tick, tock,
all day long."

Joe listens to the tower:
tick, tock.

"That is only counting," says
Joe. "I can already count."







"Then take the spare winding
key, at least," says Doc Leo.
"It has a heart-shaped bow.
Maybe that is what you need."

He hands Joe a gold thank-you
heart, too.

Joe counts it. One.







THE WINDING KEY
Pick it up (SPACE). While Joe
carries it, SPACE is a SPRING
HOP wherever gravity pulls:
one big hop, once a press.
