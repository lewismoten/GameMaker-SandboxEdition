The third goat from the left
is standing by the big music
box, looking guilty, with a
little brass crank sticking
out of her mouth.

Then she swallows. Gulp.









She came in for the rope round
a jump rope. She stayed for
the crank. Now the music box
can't play, and the toy shop's
music box is her favorite
thing in the world.

She screams about it. Briefly.








Joe looks in at the keyhole.
It is just big enough for a
small tin robot.

Inside, 7 notes have come off
the cylinder. Put them back,
and the box can turn itself
from the inside.
