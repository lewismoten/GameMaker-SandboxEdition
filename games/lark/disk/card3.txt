CHAPTER THREE
THE PARADE

Main Street, the parade
rolling, the band waiting, and
the Bard of Bizarre with
nothing to bang his drum with.









Unicycles, drum carts and
balloon carts roll east, each
lane at its own pace. They
don't stop. Bumping one winds
Joe down a little.

The Bard's 2 drumsticks went
flying when the parade began.
