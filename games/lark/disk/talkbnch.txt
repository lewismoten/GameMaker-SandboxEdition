On the workbench are the last
tin hearts Joe made, waiting
in a row. Every one of them
ticks.

None of them feels like
anything.









"I gave the rest away," says
Joe, "one in every world, to
whoever was kind to me. That
felt like something."

He writes a note and leaves it
on the desk:









"Gone to find a real heart.
Back by opening time. I will
count the steps. -J."

He puts on his bow tie
straight, and goes out the
little red door.

Step one.
