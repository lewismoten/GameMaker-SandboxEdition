Behind the little red door of
The Dog House is the Tiny
Pockets Library, and its night
librarian is Mystery Robot
Joe.

He is a tin box of a robot
with a bulb on his antenna,
reading glasses he does not
need and a red bow tie.






Where a heart would go, Joe
has a little brass tally
counter.

It clicks when he counts. Joe
counts everything.

It does not do anything else.








On his nights off, Joe went
looking for a real heart. He
looked in every world there
is.

He never found one. But in
every world, somebody was kind
to him.








So every time, he left behind
the only heart he had to give:
a little riveted tin heart he
made himself, ticking.

A thank-you. And a promise to
come back.









Somebody found every one of
them, and followed the trail
all the way to the library
door.

That was you.

Tonight, Joe is going to keep
his promises.
