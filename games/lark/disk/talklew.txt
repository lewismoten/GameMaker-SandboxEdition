"Hey, Joe!" Teenage Lewie
leans on his shovel, red hat,
red coat, red nose. "Snow day!
I'm digging out the whole
street."

"The whole street?" asks Joe.

"Sure. They're my neighbors."







"I did our walk. There are 5
more, and both sidewalks are
drifted shut. My spare
shovel's in the box by the
south sidewalk. Grab it?"

Joe counts the drifts. He
counts them again. "Yes."








LEWIE'S SPARE SHOVEL
Walk into the box, then pick
the shovel up (SPACE).

While Joe carries it, walk
into a drift to shovel it
away. Without a shovel, a
drift won't budge.








Clear each neighbor's front
walk right up to the doormat,
and step on the mat.

Lewie's porch steps come clear
when all 5 mats are swept.

The plow comes along the
street now and then. It does
not stop. Bumping it winds Joe
down a little.
