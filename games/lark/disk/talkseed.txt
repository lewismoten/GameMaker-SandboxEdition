"Joe!" calls the mustard seed
from the top of the plant, a
long way up. He is very small
and has very great feet.

"I grew this! All of it! And
now a star is caught in my top
leaves."








"It fell asleep on the way
across the sky and got
tangled. I can't reach it.
Could you?"

Joe looks up. And up. And up.

He counts the leaves. He runs
out of counting before he runs
out of plant.






UP THE PLANT
Gravity pulls down here. Some
leaves hold steady. Some fold
up and open again on their
own: yellow at the tip means
about to fold.










Halfway up there is nothing to
stand on. Hold ENTER, and the
heart balloon carries Joe up.
A dandelion puff fills its air
again.

Walk into the star to set it
free.
