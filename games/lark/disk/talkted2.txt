The arch is hung with all 12
valentines. Every dog at the
party gets one, and Gwinn gets
two, because she is sorry,
mostly.

Teddy wags so hard he sits
down.








Joe asks Teddy what he came to
ask: where could a robot find
a real heart?

Teddy brings him a valentine.
His own. It says BE MINE, in
his best paw-writing.









"A valentine is a heart," says
Teddy. "Everybody says so."

Joe turns it over. It is
lovely. It is paper.

"It does not tick at all,"
says Joe. He keeps it anyway,
very carefully.







Teddy gives him a gold
thank-you heart, too.

Joe counts: two.

TEDDY'S VALENTINE
Pick it up (SPACE). It doesn't
do anything. It doesn't have
to.
