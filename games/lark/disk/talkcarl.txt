The lamp turns, and its beam
sweeps the cove, the rocks,
the sea, and the boats coming
home.

Carl buzzes round and round
it, happy as a fly can be. He
was lost in the dark for, he
says, a very long time.
Possibly a week.






Joe asks his question: where
could a robot find a real
heart?

"Look up!" says Carl. "That
beam! Round and round all
night, for everybody out
there. That's a heart."








Joe watches the beam go round,
for the boats, for the cove,
for everybody else.

"It is kind," says Joe. "It is
not mine."

Carl gives him a spare lens
from the lamp, and a gold
thank-you heart.






Joe counts: six. Over the
hill, the road goes on.
