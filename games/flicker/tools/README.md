# Flicker and Flow: tools

- `check.ts`: the bot plays all three levels. `route.ts` stops where each loop is in view and records which frame of it the engine shows at every tick, then checks its character: the pulse torches step at even gaps; the flickering torches at uneven gaps, and the two flicker loops are at the same frame well under half the time; a flag's two blocks always show the same frame; the fall's four columns step together at even gaps while the splash's gaps are uneven; the big fire's gaps are uneven and each spark loop rests dark for much of its round. It also checks that no two sparks side by side share a loop, that every moving thing is laid on a ring's first block, that no life is lost and that no solid block is drawn like open sky.
- `walkthrough.ts`, `gendemo.ts`: re-record the walkthrough and the title demo (the bot's whole first level: the pulse, the flicker, the flags).
- `frames.ts`: works out which block of each loop a cell shows a given number of ticks in (the same way PLAYGAME does), for the previews and shots.
- `preview.ts`, `shots.ts`: PNGs of the block sheets, each level at four moments and every picture; and the progress shots (each level with Kit, the frames of a torch, a flag, the fall, the fire and a spark).
- `manual.ts`: draws the loop frames into `manual.json`'s "How this level is made" pages as small inline pictures (run it again when the art changes).
