# Lucky Cat: tools

A Special (a limited-time game): fifteen levels, 13 lucky charms; Rascal plays Earlier That Morning, Earlier Still and Wash Day (rascal.chr, rascal.cbl). Seven levels have no charms (Saucer Alley, Thirteen Steps, The Mirror Shop, The Other Side, Earlier Still, Wash Day, The Night Market): they score in saucers, ropes, keys and shakers.

- `check.ts`: the bot plays all fifteen levels without losing a life and takes all 13 charms (13 points each). It also fails on an invisible wall (a solid block drawn as sky). `route.ts` and `routeMore.ts` hold each level's orders (human timing: it reads the few signs, goes as each crow lifts off, holds Space for eight ticks, clears each cracked gap by jumping onto its low wooden landing, beats the fair's and the market's bells with a little to spare); `routeKit.ts` has the shared orders, including `mirrored`, which swaps the bot's Left and Right keys for the Mirror Maze (the level loads `mirror.chr`, whose Left key walks right).
  The cracked crossings use `crackCrossing` in `src/level.ts`: a low wooden landing over a single cracked floor tile. Midnight, the mirrored hero and Rascal all use held-rise push 12. Wash Day’s awning jumps start beside the next awning. Short ladders at the granary risers, the two rising mirror panels and the last awning let a player stop between climbing and moving sideways; the thirteen treads, mirror plinth and cracked landings remain in place.

- `jumpCheck.ts`: run by `check.ts`; every cracked gap clears in one flight with holds of 6, 8, 10 and 12 ticks, and walking across still costs luck. The five mirror pillars also clear in one flight with hold 8.
- `gateCheck.ts`: both timed streets’ gates and compact seven-bell strips stay synchronized for two cycles, with their original open/shut times. Every walkthrough world frame must show a complete strip inside the 312×196 DOS view. This checks the recorded route, not every possible detour. The strips repeat below the large bells and leave non-sky blocks intact.
- `walkthrough.ts`, `gendemo.ts`: re-record the walkthrough and the title demo (level 1).
- `preview.ts`: PNGs of each level's blocks and map, every picture on the disk, and a contact sheet of each cartoon. `catPreview.ts`: Midnight's poses. `reelInfo.ts`: how long each cartoon runs.
