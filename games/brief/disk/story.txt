Behind the little red door of
The Dog House, a little free
library on a post, there is a
library much bigger than the
box.

Every book on its shelves
holds exactly one hundred
words.

Not ninety-nine. Not one
hundred and one.




Last night the Word Counter
jammed.

It said 99. Then 101. Then
STOP.

And every word fell out of
every book.








You are MYSTERY ROBOT JOE, the
night librarian: a tin robot
with a bow tie, reading
glasses he does not need, and
a tally counter for a heart.

Step into each book, pick its
hundred words up off the
floor, and put the story back
together before the morning
patrons arrive.





Mind the shushers. They
confiscate words.

Mind Gwinn, too. She is not
allowed in the library, and
she is in the library.

...And, as always, keep it
brief!
