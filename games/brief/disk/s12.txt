CHURCH OF THE FIRST OF ZERO
by Lewis Moten
2014-02-02, Week 406
Topic: Church

Those of us who interpret the
code at the First of Zero
welcome all classes. Every
type is accepted regardless of
redundancy, complexity,
obsolescence, ignorance,
bulkiness, or style. Our
libraries are linked to our
past instructions. Through
various parameters (and
arguments), we recognize
objects by their value; and
even references. All of us
share a common interface. We
understand that some of our
inherited methods are without
exception, while others are
thrown at those who call us.
Execution is our purpose. Any
of our invalid syntax will be
judged by the great compiler.
In the end, you will be
refactored.
