THE SCISSOR SITUATION
by Lewis Moten
2026-01-04, Week 1028
Topic: Sharp Scissors

The hobo clown, Doc, swiped
the oversized,
sharp scissors from a
white-faced clown, Tippy.

Doc then ran in circles and
stepped backwards.

Tippy fell over, landing on
his big red nose.

Children laughed at the
spectacle.

An auguste clown, Mollywolly,
shouted "STOP!"

The clowns froze in mid-step.

"No running with scissors!"

The clowns ran in slow motion.

This time, Doc fell down.

He sat up and cried.

"Did you hurt yourself?"
Mollywolly asked?

Doc shook his head violently,
yes.

"I warned you!
You can't run in slow motion
either."

Clown paramedics came,
shrugged,
and carried him away in a cot.
