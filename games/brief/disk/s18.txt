RELATIVITY DETOUR
by Lewis Moten
2026-02-08, Week 1033
Topic: Vanishing Point

As Jim drew lines on his
drawing, a paradoxical view of
stairs and archways started to
form on the paper, similar to
M.C. Escher's Relativity
stairs. The tip of his pencil
came too close to one of the
vanishing points, and it
sucked it out of his hands.

Jim looked closer, confused as
to what just happened. He
tried to brush some graphite
away from the area, and was
suddenly sucked into the
drawing.

Looking at his hands, half of
them were drawn, and partially
unfinished. He looked up to
see a hole closing, peering
back into his classroom.
