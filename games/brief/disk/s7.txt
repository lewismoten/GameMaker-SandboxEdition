LEGEND AT THE WATER'S EDGE
by Lewis Moten
2009-10-10, Week 181
Topic: Fourty

Each day the programmer goes
for a walk around a pond to
relax. He sees a man playing a
flute. It echoes over the
water and fills his ears with
musical joy. A gathering of
large fish are often seen
swimming close to the man
playing the flute.

Ideas fill his head about a
fairy from the woods that
turned his wife into a fish,
and that he plays only to lure
his lost love back to him.

It is a silly story without an
end or a purpose.

After forty minutes, the
programmer returns to a life
of stress.
