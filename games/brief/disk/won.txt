The Word Counter clears its
throat.

3,200.

Exactly. It checks twice. It
rings its little bell.









When the morning patrons
arrive, every book is back on
its shelf with every one of
its words, and the reading
room smells faintly of corgi.

Joe winds his tally counter
back to zero and straightens
his bow tie.







Somewhere out front, a little
red door clicks shut on a
little library on a post.

THE END
(of exactly that many words)

...And, as always, keep it
brief!
