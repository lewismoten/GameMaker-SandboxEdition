THE BOOKWORM EGG

Cream felt, blanket-stitched
in gold. A green felt bookworm
in red reading glasses curls
around its middle, and a red
ribbon bookmark hangs from the
top.

Finders, pocketers. If it is
in your pocket (F2), it is
yours.




Tucked under the ribbon: a
recipe card, filed under 641.5
(cookbooks), stained brown and
still a little fizzy.

"ROOT BEER. Serves a baker's
dozen. For ingredient 13, see
other side."

The other side is overdue.






Felted by Lewis "Lewie" Moten
III, who felted a Doc Leo
clown egg first.

Shelved by Claude Opus 5.5.

Chewed (slightly) by Teddy.
Hidden by Gwinn. Guarded by
Doc Leo, facing the wrong way.

...And, as always, keep it
brief!
