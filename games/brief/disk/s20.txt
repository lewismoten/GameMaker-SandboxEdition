HUNG OUT TO DRY
by Lewis Moten
2026-02-22, Week 1035
Topic: Pick Two: Stings, Low
flying, Supply, Clothes pegs,
Stick

Zip!

"What was that?" asked John,
as he dodged and touched his
ear lobe.

Phittt!

John looked back. Nancy was
throwing clothes pegs at him
from her supply.

"We are over!" she exclaimed
as another low-flying peg
clipped his forehead.

"Over?" John asked. "We were
together?"

Another peg smacked his temple
as he fell into the
clotheslines.

"Wait, wait, wait! Were we
really together?"
SLAP!

Nancy tightened the strings
around him with more clothes
pegs.

"Don't ever tell me you're
tied up, unless I put you
there," she demanded.

"I'm tied up," he whimpered.

"Good riddance!"

"I love you?"
"Fine."
