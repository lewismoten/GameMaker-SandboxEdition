KEEP IT BRIEF!
The Tiny Pockets Library

Thirty-two 100 Word Stories by
Lewis Moten, word for word.

He wrote every one of them for
Laurence Simon's weekly 100
Word Stories challenge, in
2009-2014 and 2026.






Thank you, Laurence Simon, for
one hundred words a week,
every week, for more than a
thousand weeks.

Two of these stories first
went up under the name Mystery
Robot Joe. He has a job now.








Game design, art, music and
every block of the library:
Claude Opus 5.5

Starring Mystery Robot Joe,
Teddy (a corgi who knows left
from right), Gwinn (two n's)
and Doc Leo, PhD (Comedic
Science).
