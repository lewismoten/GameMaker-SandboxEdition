KEEP IT BRIEF!
The Tiny Pockets Library
Version 1.0
(c) 1995 Sandbox Software

A library game for IBM PC
compatibles with VGA. Sound
Blaster recommended.

TO PLAY
Type BRIEF at the DOS prompt
(it runs PLAYGAME with
BRIEF.GAM).

WHAT IS IT?
Every book in the library
behind The Dog House's red
door holds exactly one hundred
words, and last night every
word fell out of every book.
As Mystery Robot Joe, the
night librarian, pick the
words up and put thirty-two
stories back together.

The stories are Lewis Moten's
100 Word Stories, written for
Laurence Simon's weekly 100
Word Stories challenge, word
for word.
