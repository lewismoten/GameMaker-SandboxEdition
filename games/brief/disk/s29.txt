THE INCOMPLETE IDIOT
by Lewis Moten
2026-05-10, Week 1046
Topic: Complete Idiot

The newspaper described John
as an idiot. Frustrated, he
threw the paper down in his
lap.

"That editor is a complete
idiot," he said.

His wife, well aware of the
outdated clinical
classifications, asked, "How
does that compare to an
incomplete idiot?"

John stared at her, irritated,
trying to understand her
point.

"He called me an idiot," he
explained.

"The last I checked, both you
and he had PhDs," she replied.
"I don't think that word means
what you think it means."

John grumbled behind the
newspaper, "As you wish," and
continued reading the article
written by a complete idiot.
