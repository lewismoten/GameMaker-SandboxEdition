SILENCE HAS TEETH
by Lewis Moten
2026-03-15, Week 1038
Topic: The noisiest place in
the universe

Alone.
In the dark.

It is a cruel punishment. No
one else around. Left to your
thoughts.

With people, you could tell
them to quiet. Alone, there
are none to silence. The mind
never stops talking.

It remembers every wrong,
every injustice, every moment
no one believed.

The silence feeds it.

"Shut up!"

There are no consequences,
only echoes. A mind can waste
away arguing with itself.

When the universe is quiet,
the mind becomes the noisiest
place in existence.

"Please stop."

There is only one task left.

Not escape.
To make peace with it.

"That's enough, Warden."

Good night.
