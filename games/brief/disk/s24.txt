BUBBLE RAP
by Lewis Moten
2026-03-22, Week 1039
Topic: Bubble Wrap

I went to the store just to
kill this bore.
Looked on the shelf - yea, I
spotted an elf.
Glanced to the left, saw no
theft.
Turned to the right - got a
fright.

Peering closer,
saw a jokester.
Bubbles three,
and nothing more.

Pop-poppety-POP!

He gave a retort,
"Life can be short."

Blew through his cane -
three just the same.

One bubble big.
Two doubled small.

"Thrrrrreeeeee in
aaaaalllllll!"
he called with a drawl.

I name this Fred,
with no hope left.

I name this Holly,
still quite jolly and light.

I name this...

POP!

That poor bubble -
he's dead!
