KEYS
 Arrows, W A S D or the
 number pad: one step
 (hold to keep walking)
 F1 help
 F2 status: Money is the
    words you carry. Words
    are money.
 F5 save F6 load
 Esc quit to the menu

Walk into a door, a book or a
page to use it.



THE LIBRARY
 The reading room has a
 door to each of the eight
 wings. Each wing's
 hallway has four tall
 books on its shelves:
 walk into a spine and in
 you go.

 The door marked OUT goes
 back to the reading room.





IN A BOOK
 The words lie on the
 floor as slips of paper:
 1, 5, 10 and 20 words.
 Every book has exactly
 100.

 THE END is locked until
 you carry 100. It takes
 them, opens, and the
 bookmark behind it puts
 the story back.

 EXIT (the back cover)
 leaves without finishing.

SHUSHERS
 ...and scissors, static,
 clothes pegs and the like
 confiscate a word every
 moment they touch you.

 Short of words? Step out
 the back cover and in
 again: a book reshelves
 its words as you leave.
 The words you carry stay
 with you. Spares count
 toward the next book.



THE RESERVE STACKS
 The purple STAX door in
 the reading room leads
 to the Reserve Stacks.
 Eight egg cup gates stand
 in a row there, one for
 each wing. A gate opens
 once its wing has four
 stories back on the
 shelf.

 What is past the gates?
 Exactly one hundred
 words. Ask anyone.
