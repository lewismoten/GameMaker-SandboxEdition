KEYS
 Arrows, W A S D or the
 number pad: one step
 (hold to keep walking)
 F1 help
 F2 status: Money is the
    words you carry. Words
    are money.
 Space: pocket a small
    thing
 F5 save F6 load
 Esc quit to the menu

Walk into a door, a book or a
page to use it.

THE LIBRARY
 The night comes in four
 chapters of two wings
 each. The reading room
 has a door to each wing;
 a later chapter's doors
 are barred until the
 chapter before is done.

 Each wing's hallway has
 four tall books: walk
 into a spine and in you
 go. OUT goes back.



IN A BOOK
 The words lie on the
 floor as slips of paper:
 1, 5, 10 and 20 words.
 Every book has exactly
 100.

 THE END is locked until
 you carry 100. It takes
 them, opens, and the
 bookmark behind it puts
 the story back. That is
 the only way out.



SHUSHERS
 ...and scissors, static,
 clothes pegs and the like
 cannot take your words.
 They fine you instead: a
 point off your score for
 every moment they touch
 you.

 Overdue fines are a
 librarian's love
 language. Stay out of
 their way.



WORD LOCKS
 Late in the library, a
 brass turnstile marked 20
 guards a corner of a book.
 It turns only for a reader
 carrying 20 words. It
 takes them, and the floor
 behind it hands them
 straight back.

 So pick up what you can
 reach first.




THE RESERVE STACKS
 Behind the purple STAX
 door, a stamp gate for
 each wing stands in a
 row. A gate opens once
 its wing has four stories
 SHELVED. Past each pair,
 step on the chapter's
 stamp desk: CHAPTER DONE,
 and the next doors open.
 Then save with F5!

 Past the last gate?
 Exactly one hundred
 words. Ask anyone.
