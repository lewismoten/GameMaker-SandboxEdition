A PIRATE'S WORST SURPRISE
by Lewis Moten
2009-09-19, Week 178
Topic: Talk Like A Pirate Day!

A ship over the helm bin spyed
flying the queens flag.

The cap'n bin orderin I an' me
harties to board the ship.

I bin plunderin the seven seas
all me life.

Never did I see a dog be small
fer the number o sharp-tongued
wenches aboard.

A boxom wench twice shivered
me timbers before days end.

Harr, the treasures bin good
to me and me mates. Me harties
bin looting the cargo of sugar
and spices.

Jim Lad pried one open.
"Arrr." A large chest with no
booty.

Tis no one wanted fer tem
selves but the capn's squire.
