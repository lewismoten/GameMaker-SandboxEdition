ARROWS  walk
P       pick up (stand on it)
SPACE   fling sand (shovel)
B       throw water (bucket)
F1 help  F2 status  F4 sound
F5 save  F6 load    ESC quit

SAND: scoop sand piles, and
dig dunes by walking into them
with the shovel. F2 shows your
sand as Money. Sand plugs
anthills, fills fords and
builds towers.

TOWERS: walk into a tower ring
with sand to build it, then
walk into it again to plant
your flag. Four flags lift a
castle gate.

WATER: water balloons and the
cat's bowls fill your bucket.
Only water flips a crab. With
the bucket, walk into a dashed
outline to pack a wall of wet
sand. Crabs can't get past it.

CURRENTS carry you. Nobody
walks upstream.

CRUMBLY BRIDGES fall in behind
you. Don't stop on them!

TIDES: waves push you up the
beach, and the tidal flat
floods now and then. Wait it
out on a sandbar.

ACORNS open root doors
underground. A cracked wall
may not be a wall at all.
