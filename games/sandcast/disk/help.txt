ARROWS  walk     P  pick up
SPACE   fling sand (shovel)
B       throw water (bucket)
F       toss a tennis ball
TRICKS: S sit  T spin
R roll over  J jump
D play dead
F2 status F5 save F6 load

SAND: scoop sand piles, and
dig dunes by walking into them
with the shovel. F2 shows your
sand as Money. Sand plugs
anthills, fills fords and
builds towers.

TOWERS: walk into a tower ring
with sand to build it, then
walk into it again to plant
your flag. Four flags lift a
castle gate.

GWIN won't budge for sand or
water. Toss her a tennis ball
(F) and she trots off happy.
Walk toward her first: things
fly the way you last walked.

SPIN (T) kicks up a whirl of
sand that bowls over ants and
beetles.

WATER: balloons and Gwin's
bowls fill your bucket. Only
water (or a ball) flips a
crab. With the bucket, walk
into a dashed outline to pack
a wall of wet sand.

CURRENTS carry you. CRUMBLY
BRIDGES fall in behind you.
The tidal flat floods: wait on
a sandbar.

ACORNS open root doors. A
cracked wall may not be a wall
at all.
