THE GAME

Teddy is a Pembroke Welsh
Corgi, the knight of the
backyard sandbox. His big
sister Gwinn, a brown pit-lab
mix twice his size, just ran
through it with the zoomies:
castles flattened, hose
running, kiddie pool tipped,
toys everywhere. Help Teddy
rebuild four sandcastles, beat
the ants, the hose, the tide
and the beetles, and get the
Crown of the Sandbox back from
under his snoring sister.

To play, run SANDCAST.EXE.
Press F1 while playing for
help.

  Arrows or W A S D .. walk
  G ....... pick up
  SPACE ... fling sand
  B ....... throw water
  F ....... toss a tennis ball
            (Gwinn can't resist
            one)
  Tricks .. 2 sit, 3 spin,
            4 play dead,
            5 roll over
  F2 ...... status (counts
            your sand)
  F5 / F6 . save / load
  ESC ..... quit

Requires a 286 or better, VGA,
and 640K. Sounds play on the PC
speaker; no sound card needed.
