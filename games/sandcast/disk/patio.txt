5. GWINN'S NAP

The patio, at sunset. Gwinn is
snoring on the Crown of the
Sandbox.

Splash a paw twice and she
tucks it in: the left one by
her side, then the right one
by the crown.

Her snores blow down the path.
When the dust stirs, wait.

Her bowls never run dry. Mind
the slobbery balls and rope
toys!
