Pittsburgh, the Fourth of
July, 1995. Behind a brick
house on a hill sits a wooden
sandbox, and it has a knight:
TEDDY, a Pembroke Welsh Corgi
in a tin helmet with a red
plume.

Teddy knows lots of tricks. He
loves his toys. Everybody on
the street loves him.

He also has a big sister. GWIN
is a brown pit-lab mix, twice
his size, and she ruins
everything. Not on purpose.
She just has a lot of zoomies.

This morning she came through
the sandbox at full speed.
Every castle fell flat. She
knocked the hose on into the
moat, tipped the kiddie pool
into the lagoon, dug a hole
under it all, and scattered
Teddy's toys from one end to
the other.

Then she flopped down on the
patio for a nap, right on top
of the Crown of the Sandbox.

Your shovel lies by the toy
chest. Stand on it and press
P, then dig through the dunes
to Anthill Keep.
