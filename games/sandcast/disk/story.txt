Pittsburgh, summer of 1995.
Behind a brick house on a
hill, past the tomato plants,
sits a wooden sandbox. By day
the kids build castles in it.
By night, while the modem
upstairs squeals, the castles
belong to the toys.

Sir Grit is the smallest of
them: a tin knight with a red
plume, no taller than your
thumb. He keeps four
sandcastles and the toy chest
he sleeps in.

This morning he woke to ruin.
Lady Whiskerbane, the tabby
from next door, has claimed
the sandbox. Her kneading paws
flattened every tower.

Ants dug into the keep. The
hose was left running into the
moat. Crabs overran the tide
pool, and beetles hold the old
burrow.

Take up your shovel, Sir Grit.
Rebuild the towers, raise the
gates, and win back the Crown
of the Sandbox from the cat
who sleeps on it.

Your shovel lies by the toy
chest. Stand on it and press
P, then dig through the dunes
to Anthill Keep.
