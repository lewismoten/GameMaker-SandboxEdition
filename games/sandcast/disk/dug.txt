THE SANDCASTLE EGG

Down in Gwinn's dig, a felt
egg: sandy tan, blue waves, a
castle with a pink pennant,
one brown paw print.

Pressed into the wet sand
beside it: one word, "ONCE,"
and then nothing. As if the
rest of the story just fell
out.

Dug up by Gwinn, with help
(and a nap) from Teddy.
