THE SANDCASTLE EGG

Sandy tan felt, blue waves, a
turreted castle with a pink
pennant, one brown paw print.

Pressed into the wet sand:
"ONCE," then a measuring cup,
a ladle and thirteen little
egg shapes in a row. One more
sits apart, half dug, as if
somebody got called in for
supper.

Dug up by Gwinn, with help
(and a nap) from Teddy.
