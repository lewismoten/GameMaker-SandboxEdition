1. ANTHILL KEEP

Gwinn's zoomies knocked the
keep flat, and ants moved in.

Walk into each of the four
tower rings with sand, then
again to plant a flag. Four
flags lift the gate.

Plug anthills with sand. SPACE
flings it at ants.

Gwinn naps in the doorway:
toss her a ball (F). The can
by her has more.
