1. ANTHILL KEEP

Gwinn's zoomies knocked the
keep flat, and ants moved in.

Bring sand to each of the four
tower rings (walk into the
ring), then touch it again to
plant your flag. Four flags
lift the gate.

Plug anthills with sand. SPACE
flings sand at ants.

Gwinn is asleep in the
doorway. Find a tennis ball!
