         VIRUS BLASTER
      Version 1.0 - 1995

Your PC has a virus. Shrink
down and blast it!

GLITCH is eating the family
486 from the inside out. Load
VACCINE.EXE, climb into the
BLASTER nano-fighter and fly
up through the motherboard,
memory, the sound card, the
hard disk, up its ribbon cable
and into the CPU, and blast
GLITCH out of the machine.

STARTING THE GAME
Type BLASTER and press Enter.
You need a VGA display. A
Sound Blaster (or compatible)
plays the music and the
digitized sound effects.

KEYS
Arrows or W A S D  steer
Q / E  up-left / up-right
Number pad 7 9 1 3 diagonals
Up     speed ahead
Down   brake
Space  Nova Bomb
F1 help  F2 status
F3 music F4 sounds
F5 save  F6 load  Esc quit

On the runs that go corner to
corner the keys turn with the
track: Up goes faster, Left
and Right steer across it. On
the side-on runs the BLASTER
flies right: Up and Down climb
and dive, Right speeds ahead
and Left brakes.

The BLASTER flies forward and
fires on its own whenever you
steer. Grab N capsules for
Nova Bombs, + for repairs.
