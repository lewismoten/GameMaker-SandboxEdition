Late one night in 1995, you
downloaded COOLGAME.ZIP from a
BBS you'd never called before.

It was not a cool game.

It was GLITCH: a virus that
eats a PC from the inside out.
Mom's recipe file. Dad's novel.
Your homework. YOUR HIGH
SCORES. All going, going...

Then you remembered the floppy
your uncle gave you, the one
marked VACCINE.EXE.

VACCINE.EXE is no ordinary
program. It shrinks a pilot
down to the size of a byte and
loads them into a nano-fighter
called the BLASTER.

That pilot is you.

Launch off the floppy drive.
Blast up the MOTHERBOARD,
through MEMORY LANE, the SOUND
CARD and the HARD DISK, and on
into the CPU itself, where
GLITCH is waiting.

Hurry. It found the RECIPES!
