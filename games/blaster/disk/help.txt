FLYING
 The BLASTER flies forward by
 itself and fires whenever it
 moves. Just steer!

 ARROWS or W A S D   steer
 Q / E   up-left / up-right
 NUMBER PAD 7 9 1 3 diagonals
 UP      speed ahead
 DOWN    brake (never stops)
 SPACE   NOVA BOMB: five big
         plasma bolts at once

F2 shows your energy, your
spare ships and (counter 5)
your Nova Bombs.
THREE WAYS TO FLY
Every stage has runs straight
up the screen, a run corner to
corner and a run side-on.

CORNER TO CORNER
 The keys turn with the track:
 UP goes faster along it, DOWN
 slows, LEFT and RIGHT cross
 it. Each lap ends at a bend
 onto the next lane down.

SIDE-ON
 The BLASTER flies right. UP
 and DOWN climb and dive, RIGHT
 speeds ahead, LEFT brakes.
THINGS TO GRAB
 N   Nova Bomb charges (+2)
 +   Repair the hull (+10)
 1UP an extra ship
 BYTE good data (50 points)

DANGERS
 Turrets: shoot their armored
  collars off (3 hits) and
  they burn out for good.
 Hatches spit virus mites.
 Firewalls blink on and off.
  Flying into a live one
  burns a hole... and you.
 Data streams shove sideways.
 Bridges: RIGHT hops a lane.
BOSSES
Each stage ends at a boss
plugging the only way out.
Shoot its glowing weak points
until they burst, then fly up
through the hole. Touching a
boss burns your hull fast!

F1 help      F2 your status
F3 music     F4 sounds
F5 save      F6 load
ESC quit

Lose all your energy and you
lose a ship; you start that
part of the stage again.
