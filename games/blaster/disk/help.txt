FLYING
 The BLASTER flies forward by
 itself and fires whenever it
 moves. Just steer!

 ARROWS or W A S D   steer
 Q / E   up-left / up-right
 NUMBER PAD 7 9 1 3 diagonals
 The arrow the way you fly
   speeds ahead; the one back
   brakes (it never stops).
 SPACE   NOVA BOMB: five big
         plasma bolts at once

F2 shows energy, spare ships
and (counter 5) Nova Bombs.
SIX WAYS TO FLY
Each stage flies a loop: UP,
RIGHT, DOWN and LEFT round its
edges, CORNER TO CORNER
through the middle to the
boss, then the DATA BUS on to
the next stage.

UP AND DOWN
 LEFT and RIGHT steer. Flying
 up, UP is faster; flying
 down, DOWN is faster.

SIDE-ON, RIGHT AND LEFT
 UP and DOWN climb and dive.
 The way you fly is faster.
CORNER TO CORNER
 The keys turn with the track:
 UP goes faster along it, DOWN
 slows, LEFT and RIGHT cross
 it. Each lap ends at a bend
 onto the next lane down.

GATE SIGNS
 Along the runs that fly down,
 right and left, a sign counts
 the screens left to the gate.





THE DATA BUS
 Seen from behind, the BLASTER
 rides one of three lanes.
 LEFT and RIGHT (or A and D)
 hop a lane. No guns here:
 dodge interrupts, stray bits
 and virus, and fly into good
 bytes (+50) and repairs (+10).

 The sign counts the bus
 cycles left and the bar fills
 up. At 0 the middle lane
 opens into the chip ahead:
 steer into it!


THINGS TO GRAB
 N   Nova Bomb charges (+2)
 +   Repair the hull (+10)
 1UP an extra ship
 BYTE good data (50 points)

DANGERS
 Turrets: shoot their armored
  collars off (3 hits) and
  they burn out for good.
 Hatches spit virus mites.
 Firewalls blink on and off.
  Flying into a live one
  burns a hole... and you.
 Data streams shove sideways.
 Bridges: RIGHT hops a lane.
BOSSES
Each stage ends at a boss
plugging the only way out.
Shoot its glowing weak points
until they burst, then fly up
through the hole. Touching a
boss burns your hull fast!

F1 help      F2 your status
F3 music     F4 sounds
F5 save      F6 load
ESC quit

Lose all your energy and you
lose a ship; you start that
part of the stage again.
