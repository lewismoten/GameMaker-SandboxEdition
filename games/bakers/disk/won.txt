The Vault's lock counts twelve
ingredients, gives them back,
and swings open.

Inside, on a nail, is the
other half of the recipe card.

It says:








13. Stir counterclockwise,
keep it under your hat, and do
not tell the British.

That is the whole secret. It
always was.










Doc Leo makes the root beer in
time for the parade. THE CHEZ
pulls the stand, at its usual
four miles an hour. Teddy
rings the bell. Gwinn is not
allowed near the card.

It is the best batch in 250
years.







Twelve is a dozen. A baker's
dozen is thirteen.

You found twelve of something
to get here.

Count them again.
