Every Fourth of July, Doc
Leo's root beer stand pours
the Baker Family Root Beer,
from a recipe handed down for
nine generations.

Doc Leo is a Baker on his
mother's side. He does not
talk about it. Nobody in the
family talks about it. That is
sort of the point.





The story goes that in 1776,
Corporal Ebenezer Baker crept
into a Redcoat supply tent for
a loaf of bread and came out
with a recipe card instead.

The British never recovered.

Historians say it was the
French, the weather and
General Washington. Historians
were not there.




This year, Gwinn ate the card.

(Gwinn has two n's. The card
had twelve ingredients. She
got all twelve, and the
corner.)

There is one other copy: the
kitchen itself.







Ebenezer kept every ingredient
in the old family kitchen
under the bakery, and he built
it to keep out Redcoats: false
walls, doors that swing one
way, floors that slide, walls
on timers.

It still works. It works much
too well.






Take the rubber chicken. Teddy
will show you the stairs.

Find all twelve ingredients
before the parade.

And whatever you find in the
Vault, do not tell the
British.
