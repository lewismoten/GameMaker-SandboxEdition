WALKING
Arrows, W A S D or the number
pad: one step at a time. Hold
to keep going.

SPACE: squawk the rubber
chicken. Mice run. Toy
Redcoats run faster.

ENTER: pick up what you are
standing on.





WHAT TO FIND
Every room keeps one
ingredient on a little rug.
Take it and the dumbwaiter
takes you up to the next room.

F2 shows what you carry and
counts your ingredients.
Ebenezer paid his debts in
sassafras.






THE KITCHEN
Wooden spoons open spoon
latches. Swing doors only push
one way. Belts and greased
boards slide you. Panels rise
and fall in turn. Dough rises.
Ice thaws. Burners get hot.

Some walls are not walls.







STAYING ALIVE
Anything that is not a mouse
or an ant is one touch: Doc
Leo plays dead and the room
starts over. Five lives.

Pie: a life.
Root beer float: 250.
Bottle cap: 50.

F5 saves. F6 loads.
