THE MITTEN EGG

Fluffy white felt with
blue snowflakes in
lazy-daisy stitch, a
red mitten with a cuff
of real yarn, and a
scarf of red and green
stripes wrapped around
its middle.

Shoveled out by
Lewis "Lewie" Moten III
winter of 1992.


A note was frozen to
it:

"The Word Counter
jammed at ninety-nine.
Bring a shovel and
come quick."
