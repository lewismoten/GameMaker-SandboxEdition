THE MITTEN EGG

Fluffy white felt with
blue snowflakes in
lazy-daisy stitch, a
red mitten with a cuff
of real yarn, and a
scarf of red and green
stripes wrapped around
its middle.

Shoveled out by
Lewis "Lewie" Moten III
winter of 1992.


A note was frozen to
it:

"Bring every egg to the
kitchen before the root
beer goes flat.
All of them.
Yes, even that one.
ESPECIALLY that one."
