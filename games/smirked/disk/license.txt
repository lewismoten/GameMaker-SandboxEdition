THIS GAME IS FREE

I SCREAMED, SHE SMIRKED
is a 2026 tribute to mid-90s
shareware. The Bard of
Bizarre's 1995 shareware
notice, its fee and its
address are part of the story:
there is nothing to pay. The
song is The Bard of Bizarre's
(Lewis Moten's): lyrics shaped
by ChatGPT from Lewis Moten's
prompts; music made with
Riffusion (now Google Flow
Music); title and cover art
drawn for this game by Claude
Opus 5.5. Lily and her mom are
characters in the song.

Made in 2026 by Lewis Moten,
with AI help from Claude.

Built with RSD Game-Maker (C)
1991-95 Recreational Software
Designs, MIT License 2014.

In 1995 Game-Maker's license
let you give a game free to up
to ten people; shareware had
to ask at least $5 and pay RSD
10% of the fees. Since 2014
Game-Maker is MIT licensed,
and so is this game, except
its voice clips (below): free
to share, change or even sell.

VOICE CLIPS: NOT MIT

These 16 voice clips are cut
from recordings made on the
free plan of Riffusion (now
Google Flow Music):
AFRAID.VOC, ASHAMED.VOC,
CARDBRD.VOC, DONTRUN.VOC,
EXIT.VOC, FOG.VOC,
GETDOWN.VOC, JUICE.VOC,
MILD.VOC, NOTREAL.VOC,
OKAY.VOC, PATS.VOC,
SNEEZING.VOC, SWORD.VOC,
TARGET.VOC, WEREWOLF.VOC.

That service's terms allow
free-plan songs only
"personal, non-commercial
uses", so these clips are not
under the MIT License: don't
sell them or use them
commercially.

licensed: its code, levels,
art, text, and FM music
arranged by Claude.
