### Added

- Scoot can visit another screen: Send Scoot on a trip (on Scoot's card under Setup, or in SCOOT.EXE) saves a small trip file, SCOOT.SCT; opening it on another browser or device makes Scoot a visitor there, the games finished there stamp a postcard from that place, and sending it home brings that postcard back. A trip file holds only random ids, curated world numbers and stamps, never a name, and a file made on the same screen, opened twice or damaged is turned away with nothing changed. The graduation stamp also has a light outline now, so it reads at small sizes.
