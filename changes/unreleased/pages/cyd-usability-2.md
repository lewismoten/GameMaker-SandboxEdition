### Changed

- Pocket Player's game list now tells a first-time player to tap a game again to play it, and grays the page arrows and Details when they would do nothing.
- Pocket Player's pause menu asks for a second tap on Restart level and Restart game ("Tap to restart", for 3 seconds), and the loading screen shows a long game title on two lines instead of cutting it.
- Pocket Player's first-run hand pictures are redrawn as hands (cuff, palm, four fingers and a thumb with a nail), and Try the controls now shows a small character in the middle of the screen that walks, turns and hops as the pad and Jump are pressed.
