# Site 3.3.0 - 2026-11-09

Every change in this version (tag `pages-v3.3.0`). The [changelog](../../../CHANGELOG.md) has the summary and the most important ones.

Four games grow: The Mountain Moved Next Door (3.2, twenty-four levels, about an hour), Pass the Pie (3.3, thirteen levels, inked
art), Lucky Cat (3.1, fifteen levels) and The Thank-You Parade (3.2, fifteen levels). The Library gets its first art course and
pauses its music while a video plays, Pocket Player is easier to pick up, and two small desk and phone fixes come along.

### Added

- Art courses (#/art-courses): a first course on drawing blocks (cutting pictures, edges that meet, reusing blocks and variants so grass does not pattern), with real blocks drawn in code, on the Library shelf.

### Changed

- The Mountain Moved Next Door 3.0: a bolder printed look (carved stone, hand-cut titles and level cards, a palette for each time of day, Old Mossback's eyes following Tess), ladders and the lookout's braces that meet what they join, three new levels (Gathering Mist, Excuse Me, The Ford) and longer Stepping Stones, Empty Spot and Windy Ridge.
- The Mountain Moved Next Door 3.1: The Survey Line, the Spillway and the Bell Loft, three new levels for noon, the dusk and the small hours (twenty in all). The Bell Loft looks like the inside of a clock tower: carved beams and plank walls, a big clock face with its gears and swinging pendulum, lamps, and smoke that reads as pale puffs instead of dark bars.
- The Mountain Moved Next Door 3.2 grows to twenty-four levels, about an hour from dawn to the next sunrise: Hale's Orchard in the afternoon, The Lamplighter's Stair at the blue hour, The Records Cellar in the small hours and The Frozen Tarn before dawn, each with its own ink for its time of day.
- Pass the Pie 3.0: seven rebuilt levels with climbs, rooftops, secrets, a timed dish relay, a chase down Main Street and a three-part fight with Big Gobble before the parade starts; Rory tosses dinner rolls to stall robot vacuums and stand on them, Mr. Plume has a combo, and the game has new music and sound effects.
- Pass the Pie 3.1 has inked halftone skies, textured ground and shadowed props.
- Pass the Pie 3.2 has three new levels (ten in all): Rooftop Potluck (a fire escape, a napping vacuum for the last step and a tailwind across the alley), Sensei's Dojo (Mr. Plume kicks a vacuum into a stair and climbs a pagoda before a bar runs out) and The Dog Park (three dogs, three bones).
- Pass the Pie 3.3 has three more levels (thirteen in all): Aisle Nine (Rory hops the supermarket's shelf tops after a runaway cart), Doorbusters (Mr. Plume against eighteen shopper turkeys and a bar) and The Freezer Raid (Rory rides two fans and a stalled vacuum to the spare pie before the freezer door shuts).
- Lucky Cat 3.0: four new levels (Saucer Alley, Thirteen Steps, The Mirror Shop, Earlier Still, where you play Rascal again), 12 levels in all.
- Lucky Cat 3.1 adds three levels: The Other Side (the mirror shop's back door, a level that runs right to left on swapped keys), Wash Day (play Rascal up a staircase of awnings to tie the banner) and The Night Market (three shutters slam at the 13th chime), 15 levels in all.
- The Thank-You Parade 3.0 grows from seven levels to twelve (Bakery Row with a shutter countdown, Mill Creek, Practice Hall, the Bleachers and a Cannon Truck boss), with a page of story after every level card, donuts, a street sweeper, nine hidden playbook pages and two new songs.
- The Thank-You Parade 3.1: the Bakery Row shutter meter reads SHUT evenly, and the Cannon Truck has its own dusk street and fuse-glow colors.
- The Thank-You Parade 3.2 has three more levels (Crosswalk Corner, Frost Lot and The Clock Tower) and a short cartoon between acts, 15 levels in all, and more donuts in Call Time, the Cannon Truck lot and the finale street, so a player who takes extra hits can still reach the stage door.
- The Library's background music pauses while a video or walkthrough is open, and the AV cart's TV looks like a 1990s set (a wide 4:3 tube) with a blue-cyan vacuum fluorescent clock on the VCR.
- Pocket Player's game list now tells a first-time player to tap a game again to play it, and grays the page arrows and Details when they would do nothing.
- Pocket Player's pause menu asks for a second tap on Restart level and Restart game ("Tap to restart", for 3 seconds), and the loading screen shows a long game title on two lines instead of cutting it.
- Pocket Player's first-run hand pictures are redrawn as hands (cuff, palm, four fingers and a thumb with a nail), and Try the controls now shows a small character in the middle of the screen that walks, turns and hops as the pad and Jump are pressed.

### Fixed

- The phone's Hint Giant says Gwinn, THE CHEZ and the Round Tuit more clearly in the clips that name them.
- On the 1994 desk, Scoot's speech bubble now shows in front of the PC instead of being cut off behind it.
