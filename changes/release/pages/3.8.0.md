# Site 3.8.0 - 2026-11-17

Every change in this version (tag `pages-v3.8.0`). The [changelog](../../../CHANGELOG.md) has the summary and the most important changes.

Most Starters get the held-Space jump from First Steps, small plaques in place of long signs and a steadier view in flat rooms; Play offline lets you pick what to keep; the timed levels in Lucky Cat, Pass the Pie and The Thank-You Parade keep their whole countdown in view; and the Owner's Guide explains smaller BBS uploads.

### Added

- The Ultimate Owner’s Guide explains how to make smaller BBS uploads with DOS Game-Maker 3.0 with illustrated compression advice for pictures, cartoons, blocks, sound and text, and estimate modem download times.
- Eight more Starters declare their lessons for advisory bot checks, without changing how the games play.
- The Mountain Moved Next Door shows countdown lights beside the toe until the sidewalk opens.
- The Noodle Observatory shows countdown lights along the approaches to its five timed story exits.
