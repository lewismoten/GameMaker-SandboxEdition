# Site 3.9.0 - 2026-11-20

Every change in this version (tag `pages-v3.9.0`). The [changelog](../../../CHANGELOG.md) has the summary and the most important changes.

A fixes release: narrow screens no longer scroll sideways, Saves & scores handles storage trouble safely, Play offline reopens faster, seven Starters no longer trap you after walking left from a raised entrance, and the guided tour and screen readers reach more of the site.

### Fixed

- Players can walk left from raised entrances in seven Starters and still reach the exit without a precisely timed jump.
- The top bar stays on one row without sideways scrolling on narrow windows and touch screens, including a browser window narrower than 390 px.
- Library tile tooltips stay inside the tile, and game picture tooltips stay inside the web home on narrow touch screens, so the page no longer scrolls sideways.
- The AI setup key field stays readable on short landscape phones.
- Saves & scores shows an error if storage fails while importing or deleting a save.
- Saves & scores retries a failed backup before changing a save and tells you when an import or deletion succeeded even if the list could not refresh.
- Play offline shows saved tier choices immediately and batches cached-file integrity checks to reduce the wait when reopening.
- Partial offline copies can be removed, actions come back after reconnecting, pack sharing handles ZIP comments, and canceled archive waits clear promptly.
- The Achievements tour opens each category as Dex introduces it and shows where to reset achievements.
- On phones, the editor tour shows Assistant in the file actions menu and walks through its chat controls.
- The tour opens Teddy's game pictures, shows manual cards on phones, and points to Find, Assets and the DOS player in the editor.
- Screen reader users can navigate the DOS player settings by heading, hear the text on the wall clock, and find the modem speed controls by landmark.
- Level check catches flashing across animation loops, handles malformed acceptance reasons, and no longer reports a one-time block change as repeating flashes.
- Loaf #13 and Ohm Sweet Ohm now explain their Enter controls, and both games and A Fly Named Carl clarify their keyboard credits.
- Community game links now fit the 2,048-character limit after URL encoding.
- The exported Stick Man Stars license now names the correct game.
- The hosting guides explain how to serve missing-file responses and keep saves with the right game when reordering a list.
