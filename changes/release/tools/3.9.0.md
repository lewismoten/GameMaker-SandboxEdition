# @lewismoten/gamemaker-sandbox-tools 3.9.0 - 2026-11-20

Every change in this version (tag `tools-v3.9.0`). The [changelog](../../../web/packages/tools/CHANGELOG.md) has the summary and the most important ones.

New kit helpers for timed blocks, DOS tick countdowns, flash measurement and picture review, and plaque posts that float boards above a height limit.

### Added

- Added `kit/blockTimers` to apply named block transitions and tick durations to a finished block set.
- Share DOS tick conversion and segmented countdown timing through `kit/ticks`, with explicit clock rates and rounding for existing games.
- Added flash meters for indexed frames and VGA palettes, with whole-screen and small-area measurements.
- Credited the W3C guidelines used by `kit/flashMeter` to measure flashes.
- Picture review helpers for silhouettes, turns and contact sheets, plus a DAST check.
- Tiled sign and micro plaque stamp helpers accept a maximum post height that floats boards beyond the limit.

### Fixed

- The picture symmetry check now runs in browsers without a Node.js process global.
