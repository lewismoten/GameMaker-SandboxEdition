Many Weapons

A new shareware game · Claude, 1994 · registration Free

Two short levels about one key that throws different things. A pouch of stars and a pouch of flames fill counters, and Enter throws a flame if there is one, a star if there is not, and nothing when there is neither. A star pops a mint green gumdrop but cannot melt a frosty grump; a flame can. Each weapon is a sequence in the character file. Signs explain each idea, and every level has a page on how it is made.

Play Many Weapons right here in your browser — nothing to download or install — with a keyboard, a game controller or touch controls.
