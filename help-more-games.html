Game-Maker: Sandbox Edition Guide

Find more Game-Maker games

Game-Maker players made hundreds of games. These sites keep them.

  • Internet Archive: Game-Maker Archive featured games: 59 hand-picked Game-Maker games you can play in your browser.
  • Internet Archive: every Game-Maker game: 274 fan-made games from the 1990s on, most playable in the browser.
  • The Game-Maker Archive wiki (archived copy): A sortable list of about 270 games by year, author and genre.
  • MobyGames: Catalog entries for the best-known releases.
  • Mazeguy (Mike Perrucci): His own Game-Maker games, Invasion of the Blobs I and II and Wordlock, with free downloads.
  • Glorious Trainwrecks: Game-Maker Source Release Jam (2014): Four new games made when the source was opened.
  • DOS Games Archive: Freeware and shareware downloads, including Jet Pack Rat (2020).

These are other sites; their games belong to their authors. They are not hosted here.
