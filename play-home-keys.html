Home Keys

A new shareware game · Claude, 1994 · registration Free

Three short levels about which keys a game really needs. The arrows, Space and Enter walk, jump and throw through the first level, and a sign stands before each one. In the second, a gap looks as if it needs a diagonal key and does not. In the third, Q and E leap up to extra coins, which you can skip. A picture of the keyboard shows where the hands rest, and every key is listed in the instructions. Nothing here hurts.

Play Home Keys right here in your browser — nothing to download or install — with a keyboard, a game controller or touch controls.
