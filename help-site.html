Game-Maker: Sandbox Edition Guide

Finding your way

The bar at the top of the page leads everywhere:

  • Games: every game, to play, look inside or make your own (see the next part).
  • Guide: this guide.
  • Library: everything to read and print, by game or by type: manuals, hint books, maps, the papers in each game’s box, TURBO BUTTON, the Owner’s Guides, your notes on imported games, each game’s walkthrough, and videos about the site. Its print shop makes disk labels, CD labels and retail boxes.
  • Achievements: what you’ve done, here and on the 1994 desk, kept in this browser.
  • Cast: the regulars of the new games on a stage, and the games each one is in.
  • Desk | DOS | Web: the 1994 desk, DOS Game-Maker on its own, or this web version (see The 1994 desk).
  • ? (Guides): Show me around this page; Guides wait on this page, so two of the cast stand in its corner, ready to show you around; and Watch the whole tour of the site, or Record the whole tour as videos.
  • ⚙ Settings: Play offline, Source on GitHub, Credits, Theme (dark, light or the system’s), Guided tour, AI setup… and Back up / restore… (see Offline, installing and backups).

On a phone or tablet, the pages the bar has no room for and the settings are in its ⋯ menu.

ePub downloads

Every manual, hint book and magazine in the Library has a ⤓ ePub button beside Print. It saves the piece as an e-book that reads offline in any e-book app. A hint book’s answers wait at the back, each one a link away from its question.
